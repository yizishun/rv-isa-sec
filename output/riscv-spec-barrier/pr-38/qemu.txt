PR #38  target=qemu  overall=not_implemented
Spec: fence.hpd exception-gating via hstatus.VTSR (VS-level) and mstatus.TSR (S/HS-level) must be enforced in TCG translation, otherwise guest hypervisors and M-mode firmware lose the ability to trap and emulate the fence for nested/hierarchical protection domains
Violation:
  QEMU TCG translates `fence.hpd` as a no-op without evaluating the
  architecturally mandated exception conditions: at VS/VU privilege level when
  `hstatus.VTSR` is 1 (must raise virtual instruction exception), or at S/HS
  privilege level when `mstatus.TSR` is 1 (must raise illegal instruction
  exception). The trap gate is silently bypassed.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  fence.hpd translated without hstatus.VTSR or mstatus.TSR check; fence.hpd
  exception-gating not implemented in translate.c or op_helper.c; HPD fence
  treated as unconditional no-op; missing virtual-instruction or illegal-
  instruction exception for fence.hpd under TSR/VTSR=1
