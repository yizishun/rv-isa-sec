PR #82  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: New CSR bits mstatus.MTAG_I and hstatus.MTAG_I must be deposited from code-page PTE during trap entry and consumed by HLV/HSV/MPRV tag-check logic; missing either side-effect breaks hypervisor-guest and MPRV memory-tag isolation, allowing guest-triggered tag bypass or spurious tag faults across privilege boundaries.
Violation:
  QEMU TCG does not deposit the trapping instruction's code-page PTE.MTAG bit
  into mstatus.MTAG_I (M-mode trap) or hstatus.MTAG_I (HS-mode trap) during
  trap entry when mtinst/htinst is written with a nonzero value, OR QEMU does
  not consult these bits when deciding whether HLV/HSV and MPRV loads/stores
  generate memory tagging checks.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  helper_hlv.*|helper_hsv.*|riscv_cpu_do_interrupt.*mstatus|mstatus.*MTAG|hsta
  tus.*MTAG|MPRV.*ld|MPRV.*st|trap.*mtinst|trap.*htinst|write_mstatus.*trap
