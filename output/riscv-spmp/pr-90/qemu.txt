PR #90  target=qemu  overall=not_implemented
Spec: hspmpdeleg.pmpnum WARL re-clamping on mpmpdeleg write must preserve locked-SPMP-entry boundary or locked entries become guest-writable via vSPMP
Violation:
  In QEMU's CSR write handler, when mpmpdeleg.pmpnum is written and the WARL
  clamping of hspmpdeleg.pmpnum (which must be forced ≤ (total_entries -
  mpmpdeleg.pmpnum)) causes hspmpdeleg.pmpnum to drop below the index of a
  locked SPMP entry, if QEMU fails to additionally clamp hspmpdeleg.pmpnum ≥
  (max_locked_spmp_index + 1), the delegation boundary silently moves past
  locked entries, reassigning them from hypervisor-controlled SPMP to guest-
  controlled vSPMP
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  write.*mpmpdeleg.*hspmpdeleg.*clamp|hspmpdeleg.*pmpnum.*WARL.*lock|csr_write
  .*mpmpdeleg.*side_effect.*hspmpdeleg
------------------------------------------------------------
[not_implemented] target/riscv/:0-0  N/A
  The QEMU RISC-V target does not implement the Spmp (SPMP) extension, the PMP
  delegation CSRs (mpmpdeleg, hspmpdeleg), or the pmpnum field. A
  comprehensive search of target/riscv/ found zero references to mpmpdeleg,
  hspmpdeleg, spmp, or pmpnum. The baseline PMP implementation (pmp.c, pmp.h)
  covers standard PMP with Smepmp and mseccfg.RLB but has no SPMP entry
  concept, no delegation CSRs, and no WARL clamping logic that could be
  vulnerable to the described privilege-escape condition. The described
  vulnerability (failing to clamp hspmpdeleg.pmpnum >= max_locked_spmp_index +
  1 when mpmpdeleg.pmpnum is written) cannot manifest because the CSRs and
  logic required for it do not exist.
