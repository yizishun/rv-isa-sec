PR #75  target=qemu  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Two-stage SPMP (vSPMP→hSPMP) exception-code routing in QEMU must deliver fault to correct privilege level: vSPMP+vSPMP denial→codes 12/13/15 to VS-mode, hSPMP-only denial→codes 20/21/23 to HS-mode; misrouting enables guest-to-hypervisor privilege escalation via fault hijacking
Violation:
  When QEMU emulates the two-stage SPMP hierarchy (vSPMP followed by hSPMP)
  for VS/VU accesses with V=1 and both vsatp.MODE=Bare and hgatp.MODE=Bare, if
  the emulation raises the wrong exception code—specifically using guest-page-
  fault codes (20/21/23) when both vSPMP and hSPMP deny, instead of the
  required SPMP page-fault codes (12/13/15) directed at VS-mode—or conversely
  uses SPMP page-fault codes when only hSPMP denies, the fault is delivered to
  the wrong privilege level.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  riscv_cpu_tlb_fill.*spmp|get_physical_address.*spmp|riscv_spmp_check.*hv|spm
  p.*exception.*code|guest_page_fault.*spmp|RISCV_EXCP.*GUEST_PAGE|riscv_cpu_d
  o_interrupt.*spmp
