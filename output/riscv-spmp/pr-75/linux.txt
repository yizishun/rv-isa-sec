PR #75  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: SPMP entries now serve dual-role: host protection at V=0 and guest protection at V=1 — kernel VM entry must save/restore SPMP config or host SPMP policy silently applies to guest VS/VU accesses
Violation:
  In the Linux kernel, when KVM (HS-mode) configures SPMP entries for host
  S/U-mode protection (V=0) and then enters a guest (V=1) via sret/mret
  without saving/reloading SPMP entries, the same SPMP configuration now
  enforces access checks on VS/VU-mode accesses per the baseline Sspmp spec.
  The host's SPMP policy leaks into the guest address space, and the guest's
  memory accesses trigger guest-page-fault exception codes (20/21/23) that the
  HS-mode trap handler may not expect or correctly attribute to SPMP rather
  than G-stage page faults.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  KVM VM-entry path (HS→VS transition setting V=1) without SPMP save/restore
  or reconfiguration; HS-mode trap handler not prepared to distinguish SPMP-
  induced guest-page-faults (codes 20/21/23) from genuine G-stage page faults
  when hgatp.MODE=Bare
