PR #27  target=linux  overall=not_implemented
Spec: New Sspesa metadata CSRs (shpmsdetailsbt1/bt2 branch target PCs, shpmsdetailsdata with retire-state/CAUSE, shpmsdetailscc) extend per-sample state that the kernel must context-switch and sanitize before exposing to userspace via perf; omitting them leaks branch-target addresses (ASLR bypass) and execution metadata across process/VM boundaries
Violation:
  Linux kernel context-switch or KVM vCPU switch saves/restores shpmspc and
  shpmsdata but omits the newly added shpmsdetailsbt1, shpmsdetailsbt2, and
  shpmsdetailsdata CSRs, or the perf subsystem exposes the Sspesa sample
  record (which now includes shpmsdetailsbt1/bt2 branch target PCs) to
  userspace without sanitization, allowing a process to read branch-target PC
  values from a previously scheduled process or privilege mode.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern:
  save/restore of shpmspc without corresponding save/restore of
  shpmsdetailsbt1|shpmsdetailsbt2|shpmsdetailsdata; or copy_to_user of Sspesa
  sample record containing raw branch-target PCs
------------------------------------------------------------
