PR #219  target=xiangshan  overall=not_implemented
Spec: PR #219 removes mstatus.MXR and HLVX override from MPT permission checking (step 7 of MPT lookup); XiangShan MPT hardware must NOT read mstatus.MXR during MPT access checks and must NOT allow HLVX to suppress MPT execute-fault detection, else execute-only MPT pages become readable/writable via MXR=1 or HLVX.
Violation:
  In XiangShan RTL, the MPT (Memory Protection Table) permission checker
  incorrectly applies mstatus.MXR to override MPT-encoded XWR permissions, or
  the HLVX instruction handler bypasses MPT execute permission checks.
  Specifically, when the MPT lookup FSM (likely in the PTW/LoadUnit pipeline)
  evaluates step 7 of the access type permission algorithm, if it reads
  mstatus.MXR and treats execute-only pages as readable (violating the
  explicit prohibition 'MXR mechanisms cannot be used to affect or override
  the interpretation of permissions encoded in memory-protection-table
  entries'), or if the HLVX instruction decoder causes the PTW to suppress MPT
  execute fault checks on execute-only pages.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  mstatus\.MXR.*mpt|mpt.*mstatus\.MXR|HLVX.*mpt|mpt.*permission.*MXR|MPT.*chec
  k.*MXR|mstatus\.MXR
