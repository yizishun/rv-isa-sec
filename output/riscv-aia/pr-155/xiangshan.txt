PR #155  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Spec PR #155 assigns previously-reserved major interrupt identity 14 as the asynchronous memory buffer local interrupt (LAMBI/Ssamb); any XiangShan RTL that hardcodes identity 14 as read-only-zero or omits it from iprio priority arrays, local-interrupt classification, and delegation/virtualization logic will silently drop LAMBI interrupts, causing denial of service for the asynchronous memory buffer event.
Violation: XiangShan RTL hardwires major interrupt identity 14 to zero/disabled in mip/mie/mideleg CSR bit fields, or omits identity 14 from the AIA interrupt priority array (iprio) indexing and local-interrupt classification logic, because the previous Privileged Architecture treated identities 14-15 as reserved. When Ssamb-conformant firmware asserts the LAMBI on identity 14, the hardware fails to recognize it as pending, cannot prioritize it correctly relative to counter-overflow (13) and external interrupts, and cannot delegate it to S-mode via mideleg[14] or virtualize it via mvien/mvip[14].
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: mip.*14|mie.*14|mideleg.*14|mvien.*14|mvip.*14|iprio.*14|local.*interrupt.*14|interrupt.*idx.*13|interrupt.*14.*reserved|hardwired.*zero.*14|tie.*0.*interrupt.*14
