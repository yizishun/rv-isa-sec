PR #146  target=opensbi  overall=potential_risk
Spec: OpenSBI as RDSM must synchronize Smmtt/SmDAP domain protection CSRs with proper domain-aware fences and preserve domain isolation state across HSM suspend/resume; missing fences after MTT CSR writes or stale CSR restore on resume can break supervisor domain isolation, allowing cross-domain privilege escape.
Violation: OpenSBI (acting as RDSM in M-mode) writes to Smmtt/SmDAP domain protection CSRs (e.g., mttp or equivalent MTT registers) without a subsequent domain-aware fence (SFENCE.W.INVAL or SFENCE.VMA with domain operand), or the HSM suspend/resume path fails to save/restore domain protection CSR state, allowing a resumed hart to operate with stale or unvalidated domain isolation configuration.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: csr[wrs].*mttp|csr[wrs].*mtt|csr[wrs].*mdap|csr[wrs].*smmtt|sfence\.w\.inval|sfence\.vma|domain.*suspend|domain.*resume|hsm_suspend.*domain|hsm_resume.*domain|smdap|smmtt|COVT.*sbi_handler

[potential_risk] lib/sbi/sbi_trap.c:361-365  sbi_trap_handler
