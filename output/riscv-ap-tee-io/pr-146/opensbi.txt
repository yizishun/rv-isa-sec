Spec: RDSM Smmtt CSR writes without subsequent fence permit stale domain mappings to survive, breaking hardware-enforced security domain isolation
Violation: OpenSBI (as RDSM) writes to Smmtt CSRs (e.g., domain configuration or address translation registers) without executing a subsequent SFENCE.VMA or domain-specific fence, leaving stale TLB/domain-cache entries that bypass the newly configured domain isolation boundaries.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: smmtt.*csr[wrs]|csr[wrs].*smmtt|mscratch.*domain|sscratch.*domain
