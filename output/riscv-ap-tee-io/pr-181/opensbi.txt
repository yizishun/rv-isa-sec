PR #181  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: Failure to validate _hi==0 on RV64 or incorrect _lo/_hi reassembly in M-mode SBI handlers allows S-mode caller to forge arbitrary physical addresses, bypassing PMP/ePMP protections enforced by firmware
Violation: OpenSBI M-mode SBI handler receives a CoVE-IO call with a non-zero _hi address parameter on RV64 and fails to validate that _hi must be zero before reassembling the physical address, or incorrectly shifts/ORs _lo and _hi components resulting in a physical address different from the caller's intent.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: phys_addr\s*=.*_lo\s*\|.*_hi.*<<|unsigned long.*_hi.*!=.*0|address.*reassembl|XLEN.*_hi.*validat
