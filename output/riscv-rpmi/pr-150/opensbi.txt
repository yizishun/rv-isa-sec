PR #150  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: RPMI SYSTEM_IRQ decouples M-mode external interrupt de-assertion onto slow non-atomic transport; OpenSBI must serialize claim completion before MRET or face infinite M-mode trap loop, and HSM CSR save/restore is racy against concurrent platform microcontroller interrupt injection
Violation: OpenSBI M-mode external interrupt handler receives interrupt injected by platform microcontroller via RPMI SYSTEM_IRQ, then re-enables MIE (mstatus.MIE) or executes MRET before the SYSIRQ_CLAIM service completes and the platform microcontroller physically de-asserts the external interrupt line. The still-asserted line immediately retriggers M-mode trap, creating an unbounded interrupt loop. Additionally, during HSM suspend/resume, OpenSBI saves/restores mie/mip CSR state but the platform microcontroller may inject a new SYSTEM_IRQ mid-flow, causing the restored pending bits to either silently lose interrupts or retain stale pending bits for interrupts that were already serviced upstream.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: M-mode trap handler that writes mstatus.MIE or executes MRET before interrupt source is fully quiesced; interrupt claim via non-atomic RPMI transport where claim-completion latency opens a re-trap window; HSM save/restore of mie/mip without fencing against concurrent remote interrupt injection from platform microcontroller
