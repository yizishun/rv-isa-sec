PR #210  target=linux  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Svucrg page-based revocation guarantees that freed pages cannot be accessed via stale CHERI capabilities, but the kernel must trigger revocation as part of the page-freeing path, or the hardware-backed use-after-free defense is silently defeated
Violation:
  The kernel frees a physical page (via put_page, kfree, munmap) and
  reallocates it without first issuing a capability revocation sweep (Svucrg)
  that invalidates all outstanding user-mode capabilities authorizing access
  to that page, allowing a use-after-free via a stale capability.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  free_page|put_page|__free_pages|kfree|vfree|kvfree|mmap.*MAP_FIXED|do_munmap
  |unmap_region|free_pgd_range|pte_free|pmd_free|pud_free|__pmd_free_tlb|__pte
  _free_tlb
