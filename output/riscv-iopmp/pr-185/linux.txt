PR #185  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: ERR_CFG.msi_en renamed to ERR_CFG.msi_sel (semantic change from enable to path-select) and HWCFG2.msi_en added as capability bit: kernel must check HWCFG2.msien before touching MSI registers and must treat ERR_CFG.msi_sel as a wired/MSI selector, not a disable switch, or risk silent interrupt-path misconfiguration leading to lost IOPMP violation interrupts
Violation: Linux kernel IOPMP driver sets ERR_CFG.msi_sel=0 intending to disable MSI (old msie_en semantic), but hardware interprets this as a valid interrupt-path selection to wired mode, resulting in the kernel believing MSI is off while the IOPMP still delivers interrupts via the wired path; additionally, kernel programs ERR_MSIADDR/ERR_MSIADDRH/ERR_CFG.msidata without first checking HWCFG2.msien to confirm MSI extension support.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: ERR_CFG.*msi_(en|sel).*(writel|__raw_writel|iowrite32)|msi_en.*=.*0|HWCFG2.*msi_en.*check
