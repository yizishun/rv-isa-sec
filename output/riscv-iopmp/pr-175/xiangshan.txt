PR #175  target=xiangshan  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Wired interrupt and MSI generation must be mutually exclusive in hardware; simultaneous assertion causes duplicate interrupt delivery and potential MSI write-error DoS loop
Violation: In XiangShan IOPMP RTL, the wired interrupt output signal (iopmp_intr) and MSI write trigger are generated in the same cycle when MSI is enabled (err_cfg.msi_en=1 and imp_msi=1). The spec mandates mutual exclusion: wired interrupt must be deasserted when MSI is triggered. If the RTL's interrupt generation logic uses independent combinational paths for wired interrupt and MSI without proper mutual-exclusion gating, both fire simultaneously — a double-interrupt. Additionally, if the MSI write-error sticky bit (err_info.msi_werr) is not properly sampled and held across cycles, the RTL could re-attempt failed MSI writes indefinitely, flooding the system bus.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: assign io_intrpt = ie & ~suppress & ~msi_en; assign msi_trigger = ie & ~suppress & msi_en & ~msi_werr; // mutual exclusion missing or glitch-prone
