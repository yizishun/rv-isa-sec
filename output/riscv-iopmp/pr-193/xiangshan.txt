PR #193  target=xiangshan  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Conditional stall buffer flush on MDSTALL clear: if imp_stall_buffer is true but hardware does not flush buffer + zero stall_cntr when mdstall becomes all-zero, stalled DMA transactions are silently dropped causing permanent I/O denial-of-service
Violation: In XiangShan RTL, when IOPMP implements stall buffer (imp_stall_buffer=1) and MDSTALL register is cleared (both mdstall_exempt and mdstall_md fields become zero), the hardware must flush all queued stalled transactions from the stall buffer and reset the stall counter to zero. If the RTL fails to assert the internal buffer-flush signal on this condition—e.g., the stall_cntr register is not zeroed, or in-flight stalled DMA transactions remain latched in the hardware buffer without being re-issued—then stale transactions are permanently trapped in the buffer, new stalls may incorrectly appear as buffer-full, and the requester receives no response.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: when (imp_stall_buffer && mdstall_raw === 0) stall_buffer_flush || stall_cntr reset; grep for stall_buffer.*flush|stall_buf.*clear|stall_cntr.*:=.*0|mdstall.*flush
