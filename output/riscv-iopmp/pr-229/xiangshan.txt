PR #229  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Updating ERR_CFG.stall_violation_en during Steps 1-3 has UNDEFINED behavior per spec; RTL must either hardwire it, gate writes during busy, or flush the stall pipeline on change — otherwise transactions may bypass IOPMP checks during atomic rule updates, creating a hardware-level privilege escape
Violation:
  In XiangShan IOPMP RTL, if ERR_CFG.stall_violation_en is writable and the
  hardware does not gate or defer the write when MDSTALL.is_busy == 1 (i.e.,
  during Steps 1-3 of the atomic programming sequence), the stall/fault
  decision logic may change mid-update. Specifically, a transaction that was
  previously marked as stalled (awaiting resume) could be retroactively
  faulted with etype=0x07, or a transaction that should have been faulted due
  to buffer exhaustion passes through unchecked because stall_violation_en was
  cleared mid-sequence.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  stall_violation_en write not gated by (MDSTALL.is_busy || stall_state !=
  IDLE); stall_violation_en sampled in check path without synchronization to
  programming FSM state
