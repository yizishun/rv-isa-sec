PR #229  target=linux  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Writing ERR_CFG.stall_violation_en during the IOPMP atomic programming sequence (Steps 1-3) yields undefined behavior, potentially allowing DMA transactions to bypass IOPMP enforcement by being checked against incomplete rule sets.
Violation:
  In the Linux kernel IOPMP driver, during the atomic rule-update sequence
  (MDSTALL write → poll is_busy → update settings → MDSTALL resume), if the
  driver or concurrent code writes to ERR_CFG.stall_violation_en within Steps
  1-3 of the programming sequence, the hardware behavior becomes undefined per
  spec. This means a transaction may be checked against a partially-updated
  rule set, violating the atomicity guarantee that every transaction is
  checked either entirely before or entirely after the update.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  ERR_CFG.*stall_violation_en|stall_violation_en.*ERR_CFG|iopmp.*stall_violati
  on|stall_fault.*enable
