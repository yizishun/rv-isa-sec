PR #229  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: Updating stall_violation_en mid-programming-sequence yields undefined behavior; the kernel must quiesce all ERR_CFG.stall_violation_en writes before initiating MDSTALL/RRIDSCP updates, or risk corrupting in-flight DMA transaction fault/stall decisions and leaking stale stall policy across memory-domain reconfiguration.
Violation: The kernel IOPMP driver writes to ERR_CFG.stall_violation_en during the active programming window (after Step 1 has begun via MDSTALL/RRIDSCP writes and before Step 3 completes), when the IOPMP is concurrently checking in-flight transactions against the in-progress configuration update.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: iowrite32.*ERR_CFG|writel.*err_cfg|stall_violation_en.*=|=.*stall_violation_en|ERR_CFG.*BIT\(4\)
