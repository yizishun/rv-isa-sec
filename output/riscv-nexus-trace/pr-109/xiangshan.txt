PR #109  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: trTeDisSelfHosted bit repurposes reserved bit 10 in trTeControl to gate self-hosted trace ISA visibility; hardware must trap self-hosted trace CSR accesses and instruction decodes when this bit is set, else the debugger's exclusive TE reservation is defeated
Violation: In XiangShan RTL, when trTeDisSelfHosted=1 in trTeControl CSR, but the self-hosted trace CSRs (Smstrc*/Ssstrc*) remain accessible to the hart without raising illegal-instruction exceptions, or the ISA feature discovery still reports self-hosted trace as implemented, or the trace encoder accepts self-hosted trace commands while externally debug-reserved
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: trTeDisSelfHosted|Smstrc|Ssstrc|self.hosted.trace.*hide|trace.*reserved.*debugger|trTeControl.*bit.*10
