Spec: vabs.v INT_MIN saturation: naive abs(-2^(N-1)) wraps to -2^(N-1) in two's complement, returning a negative 'absolute value' that guest bounds checks may treat as non-negative, enabling array under-indexing or length-check bypass
Violation: QEMU TCG translates vabs.v for a signed integer element whose value is the minimum representable (e.g., -128 for SEW=8, -32768 for SEW=16, -2^31 for SEW=32, -2^63 for SEW=64) and naively computes abs(val) as (val < 0) ? -val : val, producing a negative result because INT_MIN has no positive representation in two's complement
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: abs(signed.*val|tcg_gen_neg.*abs|gen_vabs|OPIVV.*vabs|gen_helper.*vabs)
