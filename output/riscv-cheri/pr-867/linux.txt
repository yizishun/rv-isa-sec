PR #867  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: CRGE is a per-thread enable bit that redefines PTE capability semantics; if the kernel fails to context-switch it, a thread may execute under unintended Svucrg semantics causing spurious capability-store traps (Svade) or silent hardware CD dirty-tracking (Svadu) on pages not prepared for it.
Violation: Linux kernel context-switches sstatus (and vsstatus for KVM) without saving/restoring the CRGE bit, or initializes thread sstatus without clearing CRGE to a known value. When CRGE leaks between processes with different Svucrg expectations, one process runs with PTE.{CRW,CD,CRG} interpreted under Svucrg semantics while its page tables were built under the plain CHERI vmem semantics (or vice versa).
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: sstatus.*save|sstatus.*restore|__switch_to|switch_to|thread_struct.*sstatus|kvm_arch_vcpu.*sstatus|vsstatus.*save|vsstatus.*restore
