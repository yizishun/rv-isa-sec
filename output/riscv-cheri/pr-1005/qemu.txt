PR #1005  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: CBLD promoted from optional Zybld extension to base CHERI ISA — QEMU must re-gate instruction decode and capability-authorization logic under the CHERI base property, otherwise CBLD may be incorrectly exposed (capability validation available without CHERI) or incorrectly hidden (guest SIGILL on CHERI-enabled VCPU)
Violation: QEMU TCG continues to gate the CBLD instruction behind a separate Zybld CPU feature flag (cpu->cfg.ext_zybld or equivalent) instead of the base CHERI extension flag after the spec promotes CBLD from the optional Zybld extension into cheri_base_ext_name. This causes a split-gating defect: (1) CBLD may be unavailable when CHERI is enabled (if Zybld flag defaults off and is not implied by CHERI), causing illegal-instruction faults for guest software that expects CBLD in the base ISA; or (2) CBLD may remain independently togglable via the stale Zybld property, exposing the capability-authorization instruction on configurations where CHERI itself is disabled.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: trans_ZYBLD|trans_CBLD|ext_zybld|Zybld|cfg.zybld|gen_cbld|helper_cbld|decode.*CBLD|CBLD.*decode
