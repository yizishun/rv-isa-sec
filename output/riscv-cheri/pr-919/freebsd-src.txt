PR #919  target=freebsd-src  overall=compliant
Spec: sstatus.CRGE/UCRG and pte.crg/cd fields introduced for user capability revocation must be context-switched per-process and kept consistent across TLB shootdown; stale UCRG across harts or leaked CRGE across processes silently defeats temporal memory safety enforcement, turning use-after-reallocation from a trapped fault into an exploitable capability dereference
Violation: FreeBSD kernel context-switches sstatus without saving/restoring the CRGE and UCRG fields, or the pmap layer sets pte.crg inconsistently with sstatus.UCRG across harts during a revocation epoch change without TLB shootdown, allowing a process to load capabilities from pages whose revocation epoch (pte.crg vs sstatus.UCRG mismatch) should have triggered a CHERI_PTE_LD trap but instead silently succeeds because the trap was masked or the generation bits were stale.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: (sstatus|mstatus|vsstatus).*CRGE|sstatus.*UCRG|CHERI.*(PTE_LD|PTE_ST|pte.*crg|pte.*cd|pte.*crw)|CTAG.*clear|capability.*revoc|Svucrg|CRG_EXT|corrumpaper.*reloaded
