Spec: SCBNDSRD rounds capability bounds DOWN for representability to safely share buffers across trust domains; any rounding error, missed tag-clear condition, or premature implementation before the 'TODO' operation section is finalized can yield over-broad capabilities granting unauthorized cross-domain memory access
Violation: QEMU's TCG translation of SCBNDSRD incorrectly rounds the requested length DOWN (instead of correctly computing the largest representable length ≤ rs2) or fails to clear cd.tag when (a) cs1.tag=0, (b) cs1 is sealed, or (c) the computed bounds for cd exceed cs1's original bounds — any of these errors can produce a capability with bounds larger than the spec permits, enabling cross-trust-domain data exposure. Additionally, the instruction's operation section is marked 'TODO' in the spec, meaning the precise normative rounding algorithm is not yet frozen; QEMU must not implement this based on the non-normative NOTE describing a trailing-zero heuristic without formal algorithmic specification.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: (scbndsrd|SCBNDSRD|ybndsrdw|Zybndsrdw|bounds.*round.*down|representable.*length|cap_bounds_set.*round).*
