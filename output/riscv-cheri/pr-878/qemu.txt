Spec: YMODEW must clear output tag AND suppress mode-bit update on ANY integrity check failure, not just sealed-capability checks — missing this allows crafted invalid capabilities to produce executable mode-switched capabilities, breaking capability monotonicity and enabling privilege escape from capability confinement
Violation: In QEMU TCG or KVM emulation of the CHERI YMODEW instruction, the capability source register cs1's integrity checks (bounds validity, address representability, reserved bits, permission consistency, seal validity) are not performed before updating cd.tag and cd.m_bit, or the checks are performed but their results are not correctly propagated to suppress the tag and mode-bit update when any check fails
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: YMODEW.*integrity|cheri_cap_is_valid|cap_check_integrity|ymodew.*tag.*clear|ymodew.*mode.*update|cheri_cap_set_tag|cheri_cap_get_tag
