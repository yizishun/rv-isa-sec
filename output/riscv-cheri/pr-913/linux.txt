PR #913  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: Svucrg load-barrier exception priority is implementation-defined (ctag-check optional); kernel pteld handler must not assume tag-validity at trap time or dangling capabilities escape revocation.
Violation: In the Linux kernel, when the CHERI PTE load exception handler (scause=CHERI_EXCEP_PTE_LD) hardcodes an assumption about whether the hardware checks the loaded ctag before raising the exception, and that assumption mismatches the implementation, then on 'ctag-unchecked' hardware the handler will spuriously sweep pages where no dangling capability was loaded (data_corruption if it incorrectly clears valid tags), while on 'ctag-checked' hardware it may fail to trigger sweeping on capability-AMO operations where the tag arrived between the check and the handler.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: scause.*0x[0-9a-fA-F].*pte.ld|EXC_PTE_LD|CHERI.*load.*barrier.*sweep|UCRG.*CRGE.*trap
