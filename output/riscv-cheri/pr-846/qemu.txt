PR #846  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Unconditional M-field access in all capabilities leaks undefined QEMU internal representation bits from non-X-permission capabilities where the field is architecturally undefined per spec
Violation: QEMU unconditionally reads, writes, or preserves the M (execution mode) field in its internal capability representation regardless of whether the capability grants X-permission, when the spec now says the M field is only architecturally defined for X-permission capabilities and may be a no-op to update otherwise.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: capability format encode/decode accessing M field without gating on X-permission check; unconditional M-bit copy in capability move/transfer operations; M field preserved through capability bounds/seal operations on non-executable capabilities
