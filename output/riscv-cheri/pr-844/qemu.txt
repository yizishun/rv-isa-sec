PR #844  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Inverted SL permission semantics — if SL filtering is applied on capability loads instead of stores, a compromised or buggy compartment could write capabilities that survive a non-filtering store and are later loaded without the intended SL sanitization, violating CHERI memory-safety guarantees.
Violation: QEMU TCG translates CHERI capability store instructions (SCAP) using the old spec that applies SL permission filtering on loads rather than stores. Specifically, in the capability store fast-path or helper (e.g., cheri_cap_store), the SL bit in acperm is either (a) checked on load instead of store, or (b) not checked at all on store, causing capabilities stored with SL=0 to pass through unfiltered, while loads are incorrectly gated by SL.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: cheri_sl|SL_PERM|store_local.*load|cap_store.*perm_check|acperm.*filter|scap.*permission
