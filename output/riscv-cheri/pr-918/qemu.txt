PR #918  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: SCBNDSR/YBNDRW incorrecly clears cd.tag on inexact bounds encoding due to a spec copy-paste bug; the PR removes this spurious tag-clearing rule, and QEMU must mirror the fix to avoid wrongly invalidating capabilities.
Violation: QEMU's TCG translation of SCBNDSR (Set Bounds on Doubleword from Register) clears the capability tag when the requested bounds cannot be represented exactly, matching the incorrect pre-fix spec that conflated SCBNDSR with SCBNDS (Set Bounds on Doubleword from Scalar immediate). The fixed spec removes this tag-clearing rule from SCBNDSR — the instruction should NOT clear cd.tag due to inexact representability, only due to cs1.tag=0, cs1 being sealed, or cd bounds exceeding cs1 bounds. Keeping the stale rule in the emulator means a valid SCBNDSR operation that rounds representable bounds is incorrectly treated as a tag-clearing failure.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: helper_scbndsr.*tag.*=.*0|scbndsr.*bounds.*exact|scbndsr.*CAP_TAG_CLEAR|SCBNDSR.*inexact.*tag
