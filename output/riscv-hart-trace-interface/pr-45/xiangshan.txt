PR #45  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Trace encoder must auto-emit context/time on trap handler entry and privilege changes per spec; missing this logic causes downstream security monitors/debuggers to lose privilege-transition visibility in the trace stream
Violation: XiangShan trace encoder RTL fails to automatically emit context/time in trace packets for the 1st retired instruction after a trap entry (itype=Exception/Interrupt) or after a privilege-mode change (e.g., xRET, trap handler entry), causing the trace stream to lack security-critical context metadata at privilege-transition boundaries
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: trace encoder context-emission FSM does not check (Itype.isTrap(itype) OR (prev_priv != current_priv)) as a trigger to auto-inject ctype=context into the output trace packet
