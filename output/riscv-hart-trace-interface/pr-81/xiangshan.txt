PR #81  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: trcstate unbounded-wait encoding only covers WFI, missing WRS.NTO and other unbounded-wait instructions — silent trace-stream loss with timestamp corruption when clock is gated during non-WFI waits
Violation:
  In XiangShan RTL, when the hart executes WRS.NTO (Wait on Reservation Set
  with No Timeout) or any unbounded-wait instruction other than WFI, the
  trcstate[1:0] signal to the trace encoder remains at 2'b00 instead of being
  driven to 2'b11. The hardware logic generating trcstate only decodes the WFI
  opcode as an unbounded-wait trigger, missing WRS.NTO and other Zawrs/Zicond
  unbounded-wait instructions. When the hart then clock-gates itself during
  the unbounded wait, the trace encoder has no opportunity to flush accrued
  partial-timestamp branch history, violating the spec requirement that 'any
  accrued branch history must be reported within a maximum time from the last
  packet in order to maintain timestamp integrity.' The resulting trace stream
  suffers irrecoverable timestamp corruption that the decoder cannot detect,
  because the encoder never emitted a 'trace paused due to unbounded wait'
  notification.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  MuxLookup(io.hart.wfi, ...) OR when(wfi) OR wfi_signal trcstate Mux that
  does not include WRS.NTO / Zawrs / unbounded-wait condition in the 2'b11
  case arm
