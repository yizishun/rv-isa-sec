PR #2790  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: M-mode interrupt CSRs mie/mip are WARL per normative rule; OpenSBI save/restore during HSM suspend/resume writes full saved mie without post-write re-read verification, risking silently dropped interrupt-enable bits and post-resume interrupt starvation on platforms with partial bit-set writability
Violation: OpenSBI writes the full saved mie value via csr_write(CSR_MIE, saved_mie) during HSM non-retentive suspend restore (sbi_hsm.c:444) without re-reading CSR_MIE to verify that WARL legalization did not silently drop implemented-but-writability-restricted interrupt-enable bits; same pattern applies to platform suspend hooks that write mie to a wakeup mask (e.g., fdt_hsm_sifive_tmc0.c:272, fdt_hsm_andes_atcsmu.c:153)
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: csr_write\(CSR_MIE,\s*(hdata->saved_mie|saved_mie|orig_mie)\b
