PR #2790  target=opensbi  overall=potential_risk
Spec: mie/mip are WARL — writes may be silently coerced; OpenSBI must read-back verify after mie writes and must not assume save/restore fidelity across HSM suspend/resume
Violation:
  OpenSBI writes a value to mie (e.g., to enable/disable specific interrupt
  classes during init, HSM suspend/resume, or SBI handler prologue/epilogue)
  and assumes the write took effect exactly as written, without reading back
  to verify. Hardware WARL behavior may silently drop or alter unsupported
  interrupt-enable bits, causing a mismatch between OpenSBI's expected and
  actual mie state.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  csrw mie, <val> without subsequent csrr mie read-back verification; or
  save/restore of mie across HSM suspend where the restored value is not
  validated against the written value
