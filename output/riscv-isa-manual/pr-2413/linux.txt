PR #2413  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: M-mode is the mandatory highest privilege level with unfettered machine access; the kernel depends on M-mode for trap delegation, physical memory protection, CSR access gating, and SBI services — its absence collapses all privilege boundaries.
Violation:
  The Linux kernel boots on a RISC-V hardware implementation that does not
  provide M-mode (violating the normative mmode_req rule), causing the kernel
  in S-mode/HS-mode to be the de facto highest privilege level with no higher
  authority to enforce PMP, delegate traps, gate CSR access, or provide SBI
  services.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  arch/riscv/ arch/riscv/kernel/ SBI_ECALL sbi_ecall mcounteren mstateen
  medeleg mideleg PMP pmp riscv_pmp satp_mode mstatus.TVM __sbi_ecall
