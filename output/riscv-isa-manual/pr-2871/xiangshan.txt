PR #2871  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: MDT/SDT double-trap disable bits unconditionally cleared on MRET/SRET/MNRET in Xiangshan RTL without Smdbltrp/Ssdbltrp extension presence check, violating spec requirement that this behavior is extension-qualified
Violation: In Xiangshan RTL, MRET (MretEventModule), SRET-executed-in-M-mode (SretEventModule), and MNRET (MNretEventModule) unconditionally clear mstatus.MDT, mstatus.SDT, and vsstatus.SDT during return operations without checking whether Smdbltrp/Ssdbltrp extensions are actually implemented in the SoC configuration. No HasSmdbltrp/HasSsdbltrp parameter exists anywhere in the codebase to gate these CSR field manipulations. The MDT bit is defined as CSRRWField(42) in MstatusBundle with no extension-dependent reset or write-enable gating, and SDT is similarly CSRRWField(24) in mstatus and CSRWARLField(24) in sstatus. The DTE field in menvcfg/henvcfg only gates S-mode SDT behavior, not M-mode MDT behavior.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: out.mstatus.bits.MDT := 0.U; out.mstatus.bits.SDT := ... 0.U; CSRRWField ... MDT; HasSmdbltrp; HasSsdbltrp; smdbltrp absent from Parameters
