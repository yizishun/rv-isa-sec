PR #2608  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: OpenSBI must zero sstateen* and hstateen* CSRs after programming mstateen* CSRs, per the normative mstateen_zero_initialization and hstateen_sstateen_zero_initialization rules; failure lets stale sstateen* values grant U-mode access to M-mode-restricted extension state, defeating the covert-channel mitigation.
Violation:
  OpenSBI sets bits in mstateen* CSRs during cold/warm boot to grant S-mode
  access to extension state, but neglects to explicitly zero the corresponding
  writable bits in sstateen* (and hstateen* if H-extension present) before
  handing off to S-mode. The sstateen* CSRs may retain stale non-zero values
  from a prior boot or warm reset, allowing S-mode to inadvertently or
  maliciously grant U-mode access to state that M-mode firmware intended to be
  controlled solely by M-mode.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  csrw mstateen followed by MRET/SRET without corresponding csrw zero to
  sstateen and hstateen; or CSR save/restore in HSM suspend that omits
  sstateen*/hstateen* after restoring mstateen*
