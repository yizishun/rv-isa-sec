PR #2901  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Zisb speculation barrier instructions (TEB/MDB/DPB) encoded via Zimop may be silently treated as no-ops without enforcing microarchitectural speculation boundaries, allowing Spectre-class transient execution attacks to bypass software-inserted barriers and leak secrets through cache-based side channels
Violation:
  When XiangShan implements Zimop (treating TEB/MDB/DPB as functional NOPs via
  the Zimop encoding) but does NOT implement Zisb barrier semantics in the
  pipeline control logic. Specifically: (1) Decoder maps TEB/MDB/DPB to no-op
  micro-ops without asserting a speculation barrier control signal; (2) Branch
  predictor continues to speculatively fetch/execute instructions after TEB
  before the preceding branch resolves; (3) Load-Store unit permits a load
  after MDB to bypass an older unresolved store's address check; (4) Execution
  units permit instructions after DPB to consume data-value predictions from
  instructions before DPB. In each case, the hardware functionally executes
  correctly (instructions retire as NOPs) but fails to enforce the
  microarchitectural speculation boundaries mandated by the spec.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern:
  (TEB|MDB|DPB|Zisb|speculation.barrier|Zimop.*NOP|decode.*mayBeNop|isZimop|fe
  ncei.*barrier|speculation.*block|ctrl.*stall.*barrier)
