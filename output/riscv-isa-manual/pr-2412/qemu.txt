PR #2412  target=qemu  overall=bug_confirmed
Spec: Zilsd 64-bit loads/stores on RV32 use aligned even/odd register pairs; QEMU must atomically perform the combined 64-bit memory transfer in TCG, validate register-pair alignment at translate time, and ensure no stale upper-half register state is observable on mid-instruction traps — failure risks torn reads/writes under multi-hart SMP and guest-visible data corruption.
Violation: QEMU TCG translates a Zilsd 64-bit load/store (LD/SD/C.LD/C.SD/C.LDSP/C.SDSP) on RV32 as two independent 32-bit memory accesses without enforcing atomicity of the combined 64-bit transfer, OR fails to validate that the register-pair operand (rd/rs2) is even-numbered per the [[norm:Zilsd_reg_pairs]] rule, OR exposes stale data in the upper half of the destination register pair when a trap occurs mid-execution of a split 64-bit load.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: tcg_gen_qemu_ld_i32.*tcg_gen_qemu_st_i32.*paired|gen_load.*riscv_ld.*rv32.*pair|MO_TEU64.*RV32|require_rv32.*get_xl_max.*Zilsd|trans_ld.*r2_pair|trans_sd.*r2_pair|riscv_cpu_do_interrupt.*xtval.*Zilsd
