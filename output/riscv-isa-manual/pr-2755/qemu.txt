PR #2755  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: STIP read-only in mip must be gated on both stimecmp presence AND menvcfg.STCE=1; STCE=0 means M-mode retains write access to STIP for software timer injection
Violation: QEMU makes mip.STIP read-only for M-mode writes whenever ext_sstc (stimecmp) is present, without also checking that menvcfg.STCE=1. Per clarified spec, when stimecmp is implemented but STCE=0 (M-mode has not delegated timer to Sstc), STIP in mip must remain writable by M-mode software to inject timer interrupts to S-mode. Also applies to the mvip alias path: mvip.STIP must alias writable mip.STIP when STCE=0.
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern: get_field\(env->menvcfg,\s*MENVCFG_STCE\)|MIP_STIP.*writable|~MIP_STIP|mask\s*&=\s*~MIP_STIP
