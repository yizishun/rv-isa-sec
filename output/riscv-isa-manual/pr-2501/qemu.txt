PR #2501  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: New spec requires chain-of-enable for CMO envcfg bits across privilege levels (menvcfg→senvcfg for U-mode, menvcfg→henvcfg for VS/VU-mode with HS-qualified gating); QEMU must check both CSRs, not just the immediate envcfg, or U/VU-mode guests bypass supervisor CMO restrictions
Violation: QEMU TCG translates a CBO instruction (CBO.ZERO, CBO.CLEAN, CBO.FLUSH, CBO.INVAL) in U-mode or VU-mode by checking only the corresponding senvcfg/henvcfg enable bit, without also verifying that the instruction is enabled at the next higher privilege level (menvcfg for S/U-mode, or menvcfg for HS/VS/VU-mode). The spec now mandates a chain-of-enable: U-mode CBO execution requires BOTH S-mode enablement AND senvcfg bit set; VU-mode requires BOTH HS-mode enablement AND henvcfg bit set, respecting HS-qualified semantics.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: helper_cbo_*(CPURISCVState *env, ...) reading s->senvcfg or s->henvcfg without also reading s->menvcfg to verify the privilege chain; or riscv_cpu_cbo_*_enabled() checking only one *envcfg level
