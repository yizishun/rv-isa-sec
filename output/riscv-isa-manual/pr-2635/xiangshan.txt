PR #2635  target=xiangshan  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Zvknhb-implies-Zvknha: if XiangShan decoder gates SEW=32 vsha2 instructions exclusively on the Zvknha enable signal without also enabling them when Zvknhb is set, software on a Zvknhb-only-configured core will encounter illegal-instruction traps on valid SEW=32 SHA-2 operations, causing function loss
Violation:
  When XiangShan RTL implements Zvknhb (vsha2 at SEW=32 and SEW=64) but fails
  to also decode and execute the Zvknha subset (vsha2 at SEW=32 only), e.g.,
  the instruction decoder gates SEW=32 vsha2 instructions solely on the Zvknha
  CSR enable bit without also enabling them when the Zvknhb CSR enable bit is
  set, or the ISA discovery CSR reports Zvknhb=1 but Zvknha=0
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  (isZvknhb && !isZvknha) | (Zvknhb_enable & ~Zvknha_enable) |
  zvknhb.*valid.*!zvknha | Zvknhb.*decode.*vsha2.*SEW=32.*disabled |
  misa.*Zvknhb.*set.*Zvknha.*clear
