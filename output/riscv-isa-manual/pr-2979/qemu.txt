PR #2979  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: SEW=8 altfmt-sensitized reuse of existing BF16 conversion instructions creates dual-semantic encoding where TCG may route OFP8 conversions through BF16 helpers without extension gating, leaking OFP8 NaN payloads or producing architecturally-invisible results
Violation:
  QEMU's TCG translator allows vfwcvtbf16.f.f.v or vfncvtbf16.f.f.w at SEW=8
  without gating on the Zvfofp8min extension; the existing SEW=16 BF16 helpers
  would be incorrectly invoked on 8-bit OFP8 data, or conversely, properly
  implementing the SEW=8 OFP8 path without the extension gate exposes OFP8
  conversion semantics to guests that do not implement the extension,
  violating the architectural ISA contract and allowing a malicious guest to
  craft OFP8 bit patterns that exploit any discrepancy between the emulated
  and architectural NaN/rounding behavior
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern:
  SEW=8 path in vfwcvtbf16/vfncvtbf16 trans functions not guarded by
  REQUIRE_ZVFofp8min; or altfmt SEW=8 routing mapping to Zvfbfa instead of
  Zvfofp8min in require_zvfh/require_zvfhmin/require_scale_rvf
