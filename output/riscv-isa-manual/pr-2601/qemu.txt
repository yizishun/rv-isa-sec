PR #2601  target=qemu  overall=bug_confirmed
Spec: menvcfg.CDE gate bypass: QEMU must enforce that S-mode scountinhibit/performance-counter delegation is gated on menvcfg.CDE=1, and CDE must be read-only zero when Smcdeleg is not implemented; failing this lets S-mode software control counters it should not access, enabling timing side-channels or counter-manipulation attacks against M-mode.
Violation:
  When QEMU allows S-mode access to scountinhibit or other Ssccfg/Smcdeleg
  CSRs without first checking that menvcfg.CDE=1, or when menvcfg.CDE is
  writable to 1 despite Smcdeleg not being implemented (CDE must be read-only
  zero when Smcdeleg is absent), or when clearing CDE from 1 to 0 does not
  immediately make scountinhibit and other delegated counter CSRs raise
  illegal-instruction exceptions in S-mode.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  menvcfg.*[Cc][Dd][Ee].*(?:read.only|writ|check|deleg)|scountinhibit.*(?:priv
  |access|check|mode|S_)|scof.*(?:priv|access|check)|cde.*(?:readonly|ro.?zero
  |check)

  before the RV64 conditional block. On RV64, when ext_smcdeleg is false, the
  conditional `mask |= (cfg->ext_smcdeleg ? MENVCFG_CDE : 0)` OR's in 0, which
  does NOT unset the CDE bit already in the mask. Result: CDE is always
  writable on RV64 regardless of Smcdeleg presence, violating
  norm:menvcfg_cde_rdonly0. On MXL_RV32 (TARGET_RISCV64 with MXL_RV32),
  writing CSR_MENVCFG (low 32-bit half) additionally clobbers CDE (bit 60) via
  `env->menvcfg & ~mask`, since CDE in mask clears bit 60 on a write that
  shouldn't touch high-half bits.
