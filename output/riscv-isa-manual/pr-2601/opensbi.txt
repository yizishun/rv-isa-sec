PR #2601  target=opensbi  overall=potential_risk
Spec: OpenSBI writing menvcfg.PBMTE or menvcfg.ADUE without subsequent SFENCE.VMA(x0,x0) violates norm:menvcfg_pbmte_fence/norm:menvcfg_adue_fence, causing stale TLB interpretation of PBMT/A/D bits
Violation: In OpenSBI, when `menvcfg.PBMTE` or `menvcfg.ADUE` is written (toggled) during cold boot initialization or HSM resume flow, without subsequently executing `SFENCE.VMA` with rs1=x0 and rs2=x0 to synchronize address-translation caches before S-mode entry or return to lower privilege execution
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: csr[wsc].*menvcfg not followed by SFENCE\.VMA.*x0.*x0 in the same boot/resume code path before mret/sret to S-mode
------------------------------------------------------------

  Generic MENVCFG CSR write helper used by DTE, ADUE, LPE, SSE, PMLEN FWFT setters. The helper itself does not perform SFENCE.VMA — it delegates that responsibility to each caller. ADUE's caller (fwft_set_adue) does call SFENCE.VMA, but other callers (fwft_set_double_trap for DTE, fwft_enable_lpad for LPE, fwft_enable_sstack for SSE) do not.
