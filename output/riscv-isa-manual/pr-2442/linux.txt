PR #2442  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: Kernel reads *tval CSRs containing hardware-pointer-masked addresses without checking PM applicability in the faulting mode, risking double-masking or address mismatch across VMA lookup, signal siginfo, KVM guest trap injection, and ptrace badaddr exposure
Violation: In the Linux kernel, code reads stval/vstval (via regs->badaddr or CSR read) and uses the address for VMA lookup, signal delivery, or KVM trap forwarding without reconciling whether PM was applicable in the faulting privilege mode, causing the consumed address to be interpreted under incorrect masking assumptions
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: regs->badaddr|csr_read\(CSR_STVAL\)|csr_read\(CSR_VSTVAL\)|trap->stval
