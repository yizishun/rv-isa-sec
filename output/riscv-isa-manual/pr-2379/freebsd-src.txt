  Legacy MSTATUS_VM constants use pre-v1.10 draft encodings: MSTATUS_VM_SV39=9, MSTATUS_VM_SV48=10. The ratified SATP CSR uses SATP_MODE_SV39=8, SATP_MODE_SV48=9.
