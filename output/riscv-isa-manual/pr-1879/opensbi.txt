PR #1879  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: After mseccfg.MML is set sticky, M-mode execution is denied from any region not covered by an M-mode-only (L=1,X=1) or locked Shared-Region rule — OpenSBI must fully configure and lock all M-mode .text PMP entries before the csrw to mseccfg.MML=1, or the hart wedges on the very next instruction
Violation: OpenSBI sets mseccfg.MML=1 in sbi_hsm_init() or domain_context_init() before all M-mode executable regions (OpenSBI .text, firmware stack, SBI handler trampolines) are covered by a matching M-mode-only or locked Shared-Region PMP rule with X=1, causing the next instruction fetch to raise an access exception and wedge the hart in M-mode with no recovery path
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: csrw\s+mseccfg\.MML.*(?<!pmp_set|pmp_configure|pmp_lock).*(?:csrw\s+mseccfg)
