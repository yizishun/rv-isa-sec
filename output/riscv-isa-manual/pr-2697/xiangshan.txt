PR #2697  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Unrecognized CSR addresses 0x7A4/0x7A5 cause illegal instruction traps or decoder aliasing in XiangShan frontend, breaking Sdtrig trigger discovery and control
Violation: XiangShan CSR address decoder does not recognize tinfo (0x7A4) / tcontrol (0x7A5) as valid Sdtrig CSRs, causing either: (1) illegal instruction trap on legitimate M-mode access, or (2) CSR address aliasing where 0x7A4/0x7A5 decode to an adjacent CSR (e.g., mcontext at 0x7A8) due to incomplete address match logic
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern: CSR decode table or Chisel CSR map (`CsrAddrMap`, `CSR_ADDR_*`, `DecodeTable`) missing entries for 0x7A4 and 0x7A5; address comparator width insufficient to distinguish 0x7A4/0x7A5 from 0x7A1-0x7A3 or 0x7A8
