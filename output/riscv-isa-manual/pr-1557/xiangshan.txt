PR #1557  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: If the LSU/PMA misaligned-atomicity-granule check only recognizes 32-bit encodings of AMOs/loads/stores and omits their 16-bit compressed forms, a misaligned c.lw or c.ld crossing a granule boundary can lose its atomicity guarantee, allowing another hart to observe a torn read/write—breaking lock-free programming invariants that rely on zama16b for all instruction sizes
Violation: XiangShan's LSU misaligned-access handler or PMA atomicity-granule checker inspects only full-width (32-bit) instruction encodings (e.g. lw/sw/ld/sd/fld/fsd) but skips their 16-bit compressed counterparts (c.lw/c.sw/c.ld/c.sd/c.fldsp/c.fsdsp etc.) when deciding whether a misaligned access is guaranteed atomic within a granule. If a compressed-encoded load/store straddles a cache-line or granule boundary and the hardware treats it as non-guaranteed (or raises a spurious alignment trap), the atomicity contract breaks. This can happen if the RTL decoder sets an is_ma16_eligible signal only for 32-bit non-compressed opcodes, or if the PMA checker's instruction-type filter excludes compressed encodings.
Consequence: race_condition
Pattern: decoder or LSU logic that gates misaligned-atomicity-granule eligibility (zama16b / MAG16) on the instruction being a 32-bit non-compressed encoding, explicit checks for opcode[1:0]==2'b11 before enabling the granule guarantee, or a compressed-instruction expansion path that ignores PMA atomicity-granule attributes—look for signals like is_misaligned_atomic, misa_c_gated, c_inst_from_16bit in the LoadUnit/MisalignBuffer/PMA-checker modules
