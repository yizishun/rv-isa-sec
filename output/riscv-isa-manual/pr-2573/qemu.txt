PR #2573  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: SPMP TOR matching must use spmpaddr[i-1] as lower bound regardless of preceding entry's spmpen or A field state; deactivating entry i-1 via spmpen must not break TOR region i's address matching
Violation: QEMU's SPMP TOR address matching for entry i incorrectly requires the preceding entry i-1 to be active (spmpen[i-1]==1 or spmpcfg[i-1].A!=OFF) before using spmpaddr[i-1] as the lower bound. The spec explicitly states TOR matching is 'independent of the configuration or activation state of the preceding entry, i.e., spmpcfg[i-1] and spmpen[i-1]'.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: spmp_tor_match.*spmpen\[.*-.*1\]|spmp_tor.*prev.*active|spmp.*A.*!=.*OFF.*prev|pmpaddr.*i.*-.*1.*enabled
