PR #2504  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: VSTIP is logical-OR of hvip.VSTIP and vstimecmp signal; guest can pin VSTIP=1 by writing stale vstimecmp, defeating hypervisor's hvip-based interrupt clearing and enabling guest DoS against HS-mode
Violation:
  KVM writes hvip.VSTIP=0 to clear a VS-level timer interrupt, but the guest
  has written vstimecmp to a value <= (time + htimedelta), so the hardware
  vstimecmp comparator holds hip.VSTIP=1 via the logical-OR; the hypervisor
  cannot clear VSTIP without also reprogramming vstimecmp, leading to an un-
  cleared interrupt that re-fires indefinitely after SRET, causing an infinite
  interrupt loop in HS-mode
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  hvip.*VSTIP|kvm_riscv_vcpu_timer.*clear|VS_timer_interrupt.*clear|write.*hvi
  p.*0
