PR #2504  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Sstc changes mip.STIP from M-mode-writable to hardware-driven read-only based on menvcfg.STCE; incorrect RTL STIP writability gating breaks privilege separation, allowing M-mode to inject/suppress S-mode timer interrupts or denying M-mode its legacy timer interrupt delivery mechanism when Sstc is disabled
Violation:
  When Sstc is implemented and menvcfg.STCE=1, XiangShan RTL still allows
  M-mode CSR writes to mip.STIP as writable (legacy behavior), instead of
  making mip.STIP read-only and driven exclusively by the hardware comparator
  (time >= stimecmp unsigned comparison). Conversely, when menvcfg.STCE
  transitions 1→0, the RTL fails to revert mip.STIP to its writable-by-M-mode
  legacy behavior and fails to cascade henvcfg.STCE to read-only zero, leaving
  stimecmp/vstimecmp accessible from S/VS-mode in violation of the spec.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  mip.STIP.*writable|STIP.*readOnly|menvcfg.STCE.*mip|stimecmp.*comparator.*ST
  IP|CSR write.*0x144.*STIP|WARL.*STIP.*Sstc

[potential_risk] src/main/scala/xiangshan/backend/fu/NewCSR/HypervisorLevel.scala:73-76  henvcfg
  henvcfg.STCE WARL subtlety: when menvcfg.STCE=0, the regOut.STCE is forced
  to 0 but writes to reg.STCE are still accepted (CSRModule framework allows
  writes to RW fields). If software writes 1 to henvcfg.STCE while
