PR #2568  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: QEMU lacks explicit critical-error-state modeling, causing performance counters and timers to continue updating when the spec permits hardware to freeze them, breaking guest software assumptions about counter/timer behavior during hart-level fatal faults.
Violation: QEMU does not model the 'critical-error state' as a distinct hart execution state where instruction fetch ceases. When mnstatus.NMIE=0 and an M-mode exception triggers a double-trap (without smdbltrp) or routes to the RNMI handler, QEMU continues updating performance counters (mcycle/minstret/mhpmcounters) and timers (mtimecmp-based interrupts) because riscv_pmu_icount_update_priv(), riscv_pmu_ctr_get_fixed_counters_val(), and the QEMU timer infrastructure have no gating condition tied to the critical-error/NMIE-cleared state. A guest kernel that relies on counter/timer freeze as an indicator of critical-error entry will observe incorrect counter progression on QEMU compared to hardware that freezes counters in this state.
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern: mnstatus.NMIE == 0 AND (mcycle OR minstret OR mhpmcounter OR mtimecmp timer) increment without critical-error-state guard in pmu.c counter_update path OR cpu_helper.c riscv_cpu_do_interrupt path OR csr.c write_mcountinhibit
