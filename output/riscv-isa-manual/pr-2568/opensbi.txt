PR #2568  target=opensbi  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Critical-error state leaves counter/timer updates UNSPECIFIED; OpenSBI cannot depend on mtime or performance counters advancing for watchdog timeout, deadlock detection, or post-critical-error recovery
Violation:
  OpenSBI M-mode trap handler enters critical-error state (unexpected trap
  with no RNMI or mnstatus.NMIE=0) and firmware logic assumes mtime continues
  incrementing or performance counters remain active during this state
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  mtime|mtimecmp|time|counter|timer|mcycle|minstret|critical.error|unexpected.
  trap|double.trap
------------------------------------------------------------
