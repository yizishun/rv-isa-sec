PR #2568  target=xiangshan  overall=bug_confirmed
Spec: UNSPECIFIED counter/timer update in critical-error state: if XiangShan keeps incrementing mcycle/time post-criterr, a co-hart attacker can observe timer progression to detect critical-error entry and measure inter-hart timing, enabling covert-channel construction.
Violation: XiangShan RTL continues to increment mcycle/minstret/time CSRs after the hart enters critical-error state (mnstatus.NMIE==0 when NMI arrives), making post-criterr timer progression observable to other harts via memory-mapped timer reads or Sstc stimecmp, thereby leaking the fact and timing of a critical-error entry as a distinguishable side channel.
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern: crit(err|ical).*counter|mcycle.*crit|time.*nmi|counter.*enable.*nmi|csr.*update.*criterr|CriteerState|counter_inc.*error_state
