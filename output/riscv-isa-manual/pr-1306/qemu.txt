PR #1306  target=qemu  overall=bug_confirmed
Spec: Zvkgs vghsh.vs/vgmul.vs declare vd/vs2 overlap as reserved encoding; QEMU must raise illegal instruction on overlap to prevent silent GHASH authentication tag corruption from in-place register aliasing in TCG
Violation: QEMU translates vghsh.vs or vgmul.vs without checking whether the vd register group overlaps the vs2 scalar element group. In TCG, if vd and vs2 share register storage, the inner GHASH loop reads H (hash subkey) from vs2, writes intermediate results back to vd, and in subsequent element-group iterations re-reads H from the now-corrupted vs2 location — producing a silently wrong GHASH tag.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: trans_vghsh_vs.*check.*overlap|vgmul_vs_check.*overlap|vd.*vs2.*overlap|require_vss_overlap
------------------------------------------------------------

[bug_confirmed] target/riscv/vcrypto_helper.c:795-833  HELPER(vghsh_vv)
  The vghsh_vv helper reads H from vs2 at line 808: H[2] = {brev8(vs2[i*2+0]), brev8(vs2[i*2+1])}. It then mutates H in-place during the inner loop (lines 814-825: bit-serial GF(2^128) multiplication that left-shifts H and conditionally XORs the reduction polynomial).
