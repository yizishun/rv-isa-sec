PR #1306  target=qemu  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Zvkgs vghsh.vs/vgmul.vs: missing vd/vs2 overlap check causes silent GCM tag corruption since vs2 scalar subkey H is mutated in-place during GF(2^128) reduction and must not alias the output register group
Violation:
  QEMU executes vghsh.vs or vgmul.vs without checking that the vd register
  group overlaps the vs2 scalar element group. The spec marks this overlap as
  a reserved encoding because vs2 holds the Hash Subkey (H) that the inner
  GF(2^128) multiplication loop destroys and must reset per iteration; if vd
  overlaps vs2, writing partial-hash results to vd corrupts H mid-computation,
  producing silently incorrect GHASH outputs.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern:
  trans_vghsh_vs|trans_vgmul_vs|vext_check_overlap_mask.*vs2|require_noover.*v
  d.*vs2.*vghsh|OPMVE.*100011.*overlap|s->vstart.*EGS.*vghsh
