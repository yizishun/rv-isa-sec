PR #2673  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Missing wipe-on-read side-effect on seed CSR access leaks cryptographic entropy to guest, allowing reuse of one-time random material and breaking guest kernel RNG security assumptions
Violation: QEMU emulates the seed CSR (0x015) read without clearing entropy[15:0] and transitioning OPST to WAIT after a successful ES16 poll, or returns non-zero entropy when OPST != ES16, violating wipe-on-read and zero-on-non-ES16 normative rules
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: seed.*read|seed.*entropy|seed.*OPST|wipe.*seed|crypto_scalar_seed_csr|csr.*0x015|seed_csr
