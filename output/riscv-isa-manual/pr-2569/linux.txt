PR #2569  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: LR reservation not invalidated on context switch or page migration: SC from new thread can pair with LR from old thread, breaking per-hart LR/SC atomicity across privilege domains
Violation:
  In the Linux kernel, when a hart's LR reservation is not explicitly
  invalidated (via a dummy SC to a scratch word) before context-switching to a
  different thread, or before changing virtual-to-physical page mappings
  during migration, a subsequent SC in the newly-scheduled thread can
  spuriously succeed against the stale LR from the preempted thread, violating
  the SC-pairs-with-latest-LR-in-program-order rule across security
  boundaries.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern:
  switch_to|switch_mm|__switch_to.*riscv|flush_tlb_page|set_pte|page_migration
  |preempt_schedule|pause.*reservation.*invalidate|dummy.*sc|scratch.*word.*sc
