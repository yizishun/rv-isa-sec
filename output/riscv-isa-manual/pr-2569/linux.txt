PR #2569  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: KVM must guarantee constrained LR/SC forward progress for guest kernels; failure livelocks the guest inside spinlock/atomic paths, denying service to the entire VM
Violation: KVM (as the execution environment for a guest kernel) preempts a vCPU mid-constrained-LR/SC-loop and fails to guarantee eventual forward progress — e.g., by migrating the vCPU without preserving/invalidating the reservation, or by never scheduling another vCPU that could invalidate it
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: kvm_arch_vcpu_ioctl_run|kvm_riscv_vcpu_exit|kvm.*lr.*sc|guest.*reservation|kvm.*forward.progress
------------------------------------------------------------

[compliant] arch/riscv/kvm/vcpu_onereg.c:175-230  kvm_riscv_vcpu_isa_ext_allowed
  ZICCRSE extension (Constraint LR/SC) is exposed to guests via KVM_RISCV_ISA_EXT_ZICCRSE (line 215). The Ziccrse extension guarantees forward progress for constrained LR/SC sequences. When KVM preempts a vCPU mid-LR/SC, the SC fails, and Ziccrse guarantees the retry loop will eventually succeed provided the vCPU is scheduled again — which the CFS scheduler guarantees for runnable tasks.
