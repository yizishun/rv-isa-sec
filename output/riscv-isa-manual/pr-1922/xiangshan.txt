PR #1922  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: LR.B/LR.H sign-extension bug: AMO LR datapath may not sign-extend byte/halfword loads, corrupting CHERI capability bounds when subword atomics are used
Violation: When the instruction decoder or LSU execution path for AMO instructions (opcode=0101111) does not recognize funct3=000 (byte) and funct3=001 (halfword) combined with funct5=00010 (LR) or funct5=00011 (SC), or when the LR load datapath sign-extends based on a stale width assumption (e.g., always word/doubleword) instead of the actual subword access size specified by funct3, causing LR.B/LR.H results to be zero-extended or truncated rather than sign-extended per spec.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: LR\.(B|H)|SC\.(B|H)|funct3.*(000|001).*funct5.*(00010|00011)|AMO.*signExtend|AMO.*byte.*halfword|LoadReserved.*width|StoreConditional.*subword
