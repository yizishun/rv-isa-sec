[potential_risk] arch/riscv/kernel/cpufeature.c:320-323  riscv_isa_ext_data[] table
  ZALRSC is listed as a dependency of 'A' in riscv_a_exts[], but Zabhlrsc (byte/halfword LR/SC) is not present. If Zabhlrsc is integrated, it should be considered whether it is a sub-extension of A or ZALRSC, or an independent optional extension. Missing this dependency chain means the kernel may advertise 'A' compliance without Zabhlrsc support, yet CHERI requires Zabhlrsc. This creates a semantic gap where CHERI-capable hardware would appear to support 'A' but lack byte/halfword LR/SC needed for capability atomics.
