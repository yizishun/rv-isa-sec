PR #2922  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Ziccrse mandates RsrvEventual (eventual success guarantee) on coherent cacheable main memory; XiangShan's LR/SC reservation hardware must never permanently livelock on such regions or the PMA enforcement is broken
Violation: XiangShan implements Ziccrse but its PMA checker does not enforce RsrvEventual-level reservation support on main memory regions that have both coherence and cacheability PMAs. Specifically, when a Ziccrse instruction (e.g., CSRRS/CSRRC targeting the reservation set CSRs) operates on a memory address backed by a coherent cacheable region, and the hardware reservation tracker (LR/SC reservation station) fails to provide the eventual success guarantee—due to reservation entry eviction without forward-progress detection, or a cache coherence protocol that silently drops reservations on cacheline invalidations without retry mechanisms—the hardware violates the spec requirement that RsrvEventual must be supported for such regions.
Consequence: data_corruption
