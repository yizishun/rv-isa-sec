PR #2576  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Spec mandates SEW=32 only for Zvdot4a8i instructions; QEMU must trap reserved SEW values or risk computing dot products with mis-split sub-element boundaries causing silent data corruption in guest AI/ML workloads
Violation: QEMU executes vdot4a/vdot4au/vdot4asu/vdot4aus with vsew != 32 (e.g. SEW=8, 16, or 64) without raising an illegal instruction exception, because the TCG translator or decoder does not validate that SEW is exactly 32 before generating the dot-product micro-ops
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: s->vsew\s*!=\s*2|vext_check_dot_product|vdot4a.*vsew|OPMVV.*101100|OPMVX.*101110
