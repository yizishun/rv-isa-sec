PR #2605  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: scountovf VS-mode read access control: failing to mask OF bits with hcounteren leaks counter overflow status across virtualized privilege boundaries, allowing a guest VM to observe PMU overflow events of counters it should not have access to
Violation: In XiangShan RTL, when reading scountovf CSR in VS-mode, the read data is formed by bitwise AND of the internal OF-bit vector with {mcounteren[31:3]} only, without also ANDing with {hcounteren[31:3]}, causing scountovf bits corresponding to counters where hcounteren[X]=0 to expose the raw OF-bit value instead of reading as zero
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: scountovf.*read.*mcounteren.*(?!hcounteren)|scountovf_rdata.*:=.*OF_bits.*&.*mcounteren(?!.*hcounteren)
