PR #2606  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: csrind_xlate_vs_csrno() translates VS-mode siselect/sireg* to hypervisor-level vsiselect/vsireg* and depends solely on riscv_csrrw_check() privilege gating to enforce virtual-instruction fault; any bypass (debugger, monitor, KVM direct routing) grants VS/VU-mode raw access to hypervisor indirect CSR window state
Violation: In QEMU, when VS-mode or VU-mode accesses siselect/sireg* CSRs, csrind_xlate_vs_csrno() translates them to vsiselect/vsireg* (hypervisor-level CSRs at 0x250+). The spec norm:sscsrind_virtual_inst_fault requires a virtual-instruction exception for such access. QEMU relies entirely on the privilege check in riscv_csrrw_check() (line 5623–5628 of csr.c) to trap this after translation—if any access path bypasses riscv_csrrw_check (e.g., debugger gdbstub, monitor direct CSR poke via riscv_csrrw with debugger=true, or KVM routing that doesn't invoke the check), the VS/VU-mode guest can directly read/write hypervisor indirect CSR state, breaking the virtualization boundary.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: csrind_xlate_vs_csrno.*CSR_SISELECT|CSR_VSISELECT.*smstateen_acc_ok|env->debugger.*csrno.*0x250|riscv_csrrw.*debugger.*VS|CSR_VSISELECT.*hmode
