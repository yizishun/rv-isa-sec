PR #2974  target=opensbi  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Unconditional mie writes silently fail on implementations where target interrupt sources can never become pending (read-only-zero mie bits); OpenSBI's IPI init and HSM wait paths assume MSIE/MEIE are always settable
Violation:
  OpenSBI unconditionally writes to mie bits (e.g., csr_set(CSR_MIE, MIP_MSIP)
  in sbi_ipi_init() and csr_set(CSR_MIE, MIP_MSIP | MIP_MEIP) in
  sbi_hsm_hart_wait()) without probing WARL writability first. Per spec, mie
  bits for interrupts that can never become pending are read-only zero, so
  these writes silently fail on platforms where MSIP or MEIP is unsupported,
  leaving OpenSBI's wait loop or IPI path relying on interrupt enables that
  were never actually set.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  csr_set(CSR_MIE, ...) or csr_write(CSR_MIE, ...) without prior WARL probe
  (try-write-then-readback) to verify the targeted mie bit positions are
  actually writable on this hart
