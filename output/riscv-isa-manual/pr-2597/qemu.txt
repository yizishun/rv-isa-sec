PR #2597  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Zicfiss shadow-stack instructions require Zaamo atomic-memory guarantees; enabling Zicfiss without Zaamo in QEMU allows non-atomic TCG translation of shadow-stack ops, causing shadow-stack corruption under multi-hart SMP emulation
Violation: QEMU enables the Zicfiss extension (advertised in misa or device-tree ISA string, or decoded in TCG) without simultaneously enforcing that the Zaamo extension is also present and active. This could happen through: (1) QEMU command-line allowing -cpu rv64,zicfiss=true,zaamo=false; (2) TCG translation of Zicfiss shadow-stack instructions without Zaamo-guaranteed atomic load/store semantics; (3) misa CSR write accepting a bitmask that sets the Zicfiss bit while clearing Zaamo-dependent bits; (4) ISA string generation that advertises Zicfiss but omits Zaamo.
Consequence: race_condition
Pattern: zicfiss.*without.*zaamo|misa.*Zicfiss|sspush.*atomic|sspopchk.*atomic|ssrdp.*atomic|shadow.stack.*amo|riscv_cpu_ext.*ZICFISS.*ZAAMO|isa_ext.*SSPUSH|decode.*SSPUSH.*trans_
