PR #2659  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: mnepc CSR lacks WARL/IALIGN bit masking on read/write and MNRET implicit read, violating spec-required alignment invariants and exposing emulation-internal raw CSR state to guests
Violation: QEMU's read_mnepc and write_mnepc CSR handlers (target/riscv/csr.c:5460-5471) perform a plain load/store of env->mnepc without any WARL masking or IALIGN-dependent bit clearing. The spec requires mnepc[0] to always be zero (norm:mnepc_bit0), mnepc[1:0] always zero under IALIGN=32 (norm:mnepc_ialign32), and mnepc[1] masked to 0 on reads when IALIGN=32 (norm:mnepc_ialign_mask). This masking must also apply to the implicit read by MNRET (helper_mnret at op_helper.c:459 reads env->mnepc directly without masking). Additionally, mnepc is defined as WARL (norm:mnepc_warl) with invalid-to-invalid address conversion (norm:mnepc_invalid_convert), none of which is implemented.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: read_mnepc|write_mnepc|helper_mnret.*env->mnepc|mnepc.*mask|mnepc.*ialign|mnepc.*warl
