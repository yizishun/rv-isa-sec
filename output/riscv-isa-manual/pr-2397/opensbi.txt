PR #2397  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: PMP byte-range match takes priority over M-mode privilege exemption; OpenSBI must ensure PMP entries under reconfiguration never overlap its own firmware code/stack/data on M-mode-PMP-checking implementations, or risk self-fault
Violation: OpenSBI M-mode code (e.g., during PMP reprogramming in domain context switch, HSM resume, or cold boot) writes a new PMP entry that matches a sub-range of the firmware's own code/logic or scratch buffer. On implementations where PMP checks apply to M-mode, the byte-range match rule triggers a fault even when L=0 (unlocked) and privilege=M — because the rule that 'if no PMP entry matches, M-mode succeeds' is superseded by the byte-range-match rule: if any PMP entry matches even part of the access, the L/R/W/X bits govern the outcome. A partially-overlapping PMP entry with X=0 or W=0 would cause a fault on the next instruction fetch or stack write within OpenSBI itself.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: pmp_set|pmp_set_addr|pmpcfg_write.*(?!.*sfence|.*fence).*scratch|sbi_domain_context|sbi_hsm.*resume.*pmp
