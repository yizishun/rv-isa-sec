PR #2397  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: QEMU must ensure M-mode access to unlocked PMP regions correctly fails for partially-matching accesses spanning PMP boundaries; the spec clarification confirms the priority/matching-all-bytes rule takes precedence over the M-mode unlocked bypass.
Violation:
  In QEMU pmp_hart_has_privs() at pmp.c:435, an M-mode access matching a PMP
  entry with L bit clear returns success with full R/W/X permissions
  regardless of the entry's R/W/X bits. The spec clarification states the 'If
  the L bit is clear and the privilege mode of the access is M, the access
  succeeds' rule does NOT supersede the priority-and-matching rule: an access
  that is partially matched (spans a PMP boundary) must fail even for M-mode
  to an unlocked region. QEMU correctly enforces partial-match failures
  (pmp.c:413-418) before checking the L bit, but the sequential logic at lines
  424-517 must ensure that once a PMP entry matches all bytes, the L-cleared
  M-mode bypass at line 435 does not incorrectly override the all-bytes-
  matching requirement for accesses crossing into regions with different PMP
  entries.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  pmp_hart_has_privs.*mode.*!=.*PRV_M.*pmp_is_locked
