PR #2977  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: menvcfg/menvcfgh CSR existence must be gated on U-mode support; unconditional registration or missing U-mode check causes guest-visible CSR presence mismatch with spec
Violation: QEMU unconditionally registers menvcfg/menvcfgh CSRs regardless of whether U-mode is enabled (misregistering), or fails to register them when U-mode is present, violating the spec rule that menvcfg must exist iff U-mode is supported (and menvcfgh must exist iff U-mode is supported with S-mode and XLEN=32).
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: riscv_csr_register.*menvcfg[^h]|riscv_csr_register.*menvcfgh|predicate.*menvcfg|predicate.*menvcfgh
