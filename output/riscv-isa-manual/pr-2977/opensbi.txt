PR #2977  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: OpenSBI must guard menvcfg/menvcfgh CSR accesses with a misa.U-mode existence check, as the spec ties their presence exclusively to U-mode support
Violation:
  OpenSBI M-mode code executes CSR read/write to menvcfg or menvcfgh on a hart
  where U-mode is not supported (misa.U=0), violating the normative rule that
  these registers do not exist without U-mode. The resulting illegal-
  instruction trap in M-mode is typically unrecoverable.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  CSR access to menvcfg|menvcfgh without prior misa.U-bit existence guard;
  unconditional csr_read/csr_write(CSR_MENVCFG|CSR_MENVCFGH) in init or HSM
  context-switch paths
