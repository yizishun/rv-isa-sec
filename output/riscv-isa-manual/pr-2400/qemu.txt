PR #2400  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: QEMU TCG write_menvcfg/write_henvcfg do not flush TLBs on ADUE changes, violating spec mandate that ADUE settings are 'permitted to be cached in a TLB' and require software SFENCE.VMA for synchronization — stale TLB entries can bypass A/D page faults that real hardware would raise.
Violation:
  In QEMU TCG, write_menvcfg() and write_henvcfg() store the ADUE bit without
  calling tlb_flush() or tb_flush(). The page table walker
  (get_physical_address) reads menvcfg/henvcfg only on TLB miss, so TLB hits
  use cached translations resolved under the old ADUE value. Specifically, a
  TLB entry filled when ADUE=1 (hardware sets A/D bits during walk) persists
  after ADUE→0 (Svade: fault on unset A/D), allowing accesses through stale
  TLB entries that would fault on real hardware where the new ADUE
  interpretation applies.
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern:
  write_menvcfg\b|\bwrite_henvcfg\b.*\{ — no tlb_flush\(|tb_flush\( when
  menvcfg_cfg.ADUE or HENVCFG_ADUE bit toggles
