PR #2400  target=freebsd-src  overall=compliant
Spec: FreeBSD bhyve writes henvcfg on VM entry at vmm_riscv.c:632 without a trailing HFENCE.VVMA; if ADUE were ever added to that CSR write path, stale TLB A/D interpretations would leak across VMID boundaries or corrupt guest page-table A/D bits
Violation: In the FreeBSD kernel, writing menvcfg.ADUE or henvcfg.ADUE via csr_write without subsequently executing the architecturally mandated TLB-synchronization fence (SFENCE.VMA with rs1=x0,rs2=x0 for menvcfg; HFENCE.VVMA with rs1=x0,rs2=x0 for henvcfg) leaves stale address-translation cache entries that continue to interpret PTE A/D bits under the old ADUE setting, producing inconsistent hardware-vs-software A/D updates across harts.
Consequence: race_condition
Pattern: csr_write\(\s*(menvcfg|henvcfg)\b
------------------------------------------------------------
[compliant] sys/riscv/vmm/vmm_riscv.c:631-632  riscv_vmm_vcpu_run
  Only henvcfg write in the kernel writes HENVCFG_STCE (Sstc timer enable, bit 63), not ADUE (bit 61). STCE does not affect address translation or TLB contents, so no HFENCE.VVMA or SFENCE.VMA synchronization is architecturally required after this write. The preceding hfence_gvma() at line 628 is documented as being for the hgatp write, not henvcfg.

  | 		csr_write(henvcfg, HENVCFG_STCE);
