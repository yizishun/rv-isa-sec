PR #3000  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: FCVT.D.S must NOT share the integer-range validity check with FCVT.W[U]/L[U].S; misapplying the int_conv table to double-to-single conversion causes spurious NV exceptions and corrupted conversion results
Violation: In XiangShan RTL, the FPU conversion pipeline incorrectly applies the integer-range validity check from the int_conv table (designed for FCVT.W[U].S and FCVT.L[U].S) to the FCVT.D.S (double-to-single) instruction. Specifically, the fcvtds decoder maps to the same validity-checking micro-op or the FCVT.D.S execution unit shares a common valid-input-range comparator with the integer-conversion FCVT instructions, causing a double-precision floating-point operand to be compared against the 32-bit or 64-bit signed/unsigned integer representable range. When the double-precision value lies outside the integer range (e.g., 1e100, NaN, infinity), the hardware incorrectly asserts the NV (Invalid Operation) flag in fflags and writes the clipped integer-bound value instead of the correctly rounded single-precision result.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: FCVT\.D\.S.*valid.*input|fcvt.*int_conv|FCVT.*range_check.*int|fcvtds.*nv_flag|FCVT_D_S.*fp_to_int.*validity
