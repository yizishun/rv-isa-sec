PR #2599  target=linux  overall=potential_risk
Spec: KVM G-stage PTE walkers unaware of Svnapot may incompletely write-protect or unmap NAPOT G-stage mappings, leaving a guest with writable access to memory the hypervisor intended to protect
Violation: KVM G-stage PTE manipulation (write-protect, unmap, dirty/accessed tracking) iterates at PAGE_SIZE granularity and ignores the NAPOT PTE format in G-stage tables; a single NAPOT PTE covering 64 KiB is treated as a 4 KiB entry, so the remaining 60 KiB escape the intended protection change or invalidation
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: kvm_riscv_gstage_wp_range|kvm_riscv_gstage_unmap_range|kvm_riscv_gstage_set_pte|for.*addr.*PAGE_SIZE.*gstage
------------------------------------------------------------
