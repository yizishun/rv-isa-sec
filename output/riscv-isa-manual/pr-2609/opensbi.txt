PR #2609  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: Silent mseccfg.MSSE write failure when RLB=0 with locked PMP entries disables M-mode shadow stack enforcement, breaking assumed backward-edge CFI
Violation:
  OpenSBI writes mseccfg.MSSE=1 to enable M-mode shadow stack, but
  mseccfg.RLB=0 and at least one PMP entry has pmpcfg.L=1, causing the MSSE
  write to be silently ignored per spec. OpenSBI then executes shadow stack
  instructions (sspush/sspopchk) assuming SSE enforcement is active, but no
  CFI protection is actually applied.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  csr_write.*CSR_MSECCFG|CSR_MSECCFG.*MSSE|mseccfg.*write
