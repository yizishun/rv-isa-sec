PR #2609  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Extended PMP E-field shadow-stack semantics bypass: QEMU PMP checker must recognize E=1 pmpcfg entries as defining shadow-stack regions with restricted access rules distinct from normal R/W/X, otherwise guest CFI enforcement is silently nullified by the emulator.
Violation:
  When a PMP entry has pmpcfg.E=1 (bit MXLEN-1) and XWR encoding corresponds
  to a shadow-stack region (e.g., XWR=010 for M-mode under Smcfiss, XWR=110
  for U-mode under Smucfiss), but QEMU's pmp_hart_has_privs() treats the XWR
  bits as normal R/W/X permissions — allowing non-shadow-stack instructions to
  write to the region and permitting implicit accesses (instruction fetches,
  page table walks) without raising access-fault exceptions as required by the
  spec.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  pmp_hart_has_privs() or pmp_hart_has_privs_default() in target/riscv/pmp.c
  does not examine the E (extended-attributes) field at bit (MXLEN-1) of
  pmpcfg prior to evaluating XWR permissions, and does not classify XWR=010
  (M-mode SS) or XWR=110 (U-mode SS) as shadow-stack regions requiring
  distinct access enforcement (store denied for non-SS instructions, implicit
  accesses fault, SS instructions restricted to SS regions only).
