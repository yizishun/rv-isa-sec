PR #2091  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Incorrect LSQ memory ordering enforcement for Zalasr load-acquire/store-release instructions enables race conditions in lock-based synchronization, directly undermining mutual exclusion and atomicity guarantees in multi-hart XiangShan configurations
Violation: XiangShan's out-of-order LSU/LSQ fails to enforce acquire-release memory ordering for Zalasr instructions: (1) a younger store or load is allowed to commit before an older load-acquire completes (acquire violated), OR (2) a younger store-release is allowed to commit before older loads/stores drain from the LSQ (release violated). This occurs when the LSQ issue logic treats Zalasr loads/stores as ordinary loads/stores without inserting the required unidirectional memory barrier pipeline bubbles, or when the mem-dep predictor allows speculative reordering across aq/rl-annotated accesses without recovery on mis-speculation.
Consequence: race_condition
Pattern: funct5 === 00110 (load-acquire) OR funct5 === 00111 (store-release) without corresponding LSQ acquire/release barrier insertion; AMO opcode load/store path without aq/rl ordering enforcement in LoadQueue/ScheduleMem stage; mem_forwarding across aq/rl boundary without ordering check
