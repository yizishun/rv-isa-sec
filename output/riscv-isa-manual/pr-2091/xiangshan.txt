PR #2091  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Zalasr introduces load-acquire (funct5=00110, mandatory aq=1) and store-release (funct5=00111, mandatory rl=1) under the AMO opcode; RESERVED encodings (aq=0 or rl=0) must trap via illegal-instruction exception, and RV64-only variants (ld/sd) must be rejected on RV32; hardware decoder rejection of reserved encodings and OoO LSU enforcement of acquire-release RCsc ordering are both security-critical implementation requirements
Violation:
  XiangShan's instruction decoder funt5 comparator for the AMO opcode space
  fails to reject RESERVED encodings of Zalasr: (a) load-acquire with aq=0
  (funct5=00110, aq=0) yields an undefined instruction that is decoded and
  executed rather than raising an illegal-instruction exception; (b) store-
  release with rl=0 (funct5=00111, rl=0) is similarly executed; (c) on
  RV64-only XiangShan configurations, ld.{aq,aqrl} or sd.{rl,aqrl} may be
  decoded and partially executed on a 32-bit datapath. Additionally, in the
  out-of-order LSU (Load-Store Unit), the acquire-RCsc ordering annotation (aq
  bit) is not propagated to the load-store queue, so younger loads/stores are
  permitted to commit before the acquire-load completes, violating the
  unidirectional fence semantics required by RISC-V RVWMO and creating
  architecturally observable reordering.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:

  ecode.*funct5.*00110|AMO_0101111.*decode.*funct5.*00111|load_acquire.*aq_bit
  |store_release.*rl_bit|aq.*rl.*ordering|acquire.*release.*RCsc|load.*acquire
  .*reorder|store.*release.*reorder|Zalasr.*decode|funct5.*00110.*00111.*RESER
  VED
