PR #2868  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Splitting norm:constrained_lrsc_forward_progress into five granular sub-tags (intro, sc, store_amo, branch, trap) clarifies that QEMU must not spuriously invalidate LR reservations—any reservation loss without an architected store, successful SC, branch exit, or trap constitutes a forward-progress denial-of-service against the guest hart's constrained LR/SC loop
Violation: In QEMU TCG multi-hart emulation, when a guest hart H enters a constrained LR/SC loop, QEMU must guarantee forward progress via one of: (a) successful SC by any hart on H's reservation set, (b) unconditional store/AMO from another hart/device on the reservation set, (c) H branching out of the loop, or (d) H trapping. This guarantee is violated if QEMU's reservation tracking state for H is spuriously cleared without any of the above events occurring—for example, if the per-hart load reservation is lost on an unrelated CSR write, on an exception entry/return that incorrectly resets reservation state, or if TCG register allocation causes the reservation monitor to be silently invalidated without architected side-effects completing.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: helper_lr_d\b|helper_sc_d\b|cpu->load_res|cpu->load_val|reservation.*clear|LR.*reservation.*reset|load_res.*=.*-1|RISCVCPU.*load_res

[potential_risk] target/riscv/insn_trans/trans_rva.c.inc:72-82  gen_sc
  SC uses load_res (the reservation address) as the addr parameter to tcg_gen_atomic_cmpxchg_tl and load_val (the loaded data) as the cmp (expected old value). This is correct. However, QEMU TCG does NOT implement cross-hart reservation invalidation: a store/AMO from hart B to hart A's reservation set does NOT clear hart A's load_res. This is a known limitation of QEMU's simplified per-hart reservation model. The RISC-V spec requires SC to fail if another hart stores to the reservation set. This could violate the forward-progress guarantee in constrained LR/SC loops under multi-hart TCG emulation.
