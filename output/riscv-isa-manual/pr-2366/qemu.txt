PR #2366  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: STIP read-only in mip/sip must be gated on menvcfg.STCE==1, not merely on ext_sstc presence; otherwise guest OS timer interrupts are lost when firmware disables STCE and falls back to the legacy M-mode STIP-write delivery path
Violation:
  QEMU's rmw_mip64 makes STIP read-only in mip (or rmw_mvip64 strips STIP from
  sip's mip alias) based solely on ext_sstc==true, without also checking that
  menvcfg.STCE==1. When STCE=0 but sstc is implemented, the spec requires STIP
  to be writable by M-mode in mip and to alias into sip, so that M-mode
  firmware (OpenSBI) can deliver timer interrupts to S-mode via the legacy
  STIP-write mechanism. Omitting the STCE check permanently locks STIP read-
  only, blocking the fallback timer interrupt path.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  if.*ext_sstc.*mask.*~MIP_STIP without a preceding or combined
  get_field.*menvcfg.*MENVCFG_STCE check in rmw_mip64 or rmw_mvip64; or a
  riscv_timer_write_timecmp path that drives STIP based on stimecmp comparison
  without first checking menvcfg.STCE
