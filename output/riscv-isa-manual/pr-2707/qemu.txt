PR #2707  target=qemu  overall=bug_confirmed
Spec: Missing or incomplete WARL bit-masking in QEMU's hypervisor, supervisor, and RNMI CSR emulation allows guest reads to observe unwritable bits that the spec mandates as read-only zero, leaking implementation details about which interrupt types and delegation paths are supported
Violation: QEMU's CSR read/write handlers for sip/sie, vsip/vsie, hip/hie, hvip, hedeleg, hideleg, hgeie, stvec/vstvec, mnepc, mncause, or mnstatus do not apply per-bit WARL masking on writes (silently dropping writes to read-only-zero bits) and do not apply WARL legalization on reads (returning only the constrained legal value). A guest write to an unimplemented interrupt bit in sie that persists on read-back, or a write to a read-only-zero delegation bit in hedeleg/hideleg that is not silently ignored, violates the spec's WARL contract.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: riscv_csr_read|riscv_csr_write.*(sip|sie|vsip|vsie|hip|hie|hvip|hedeleg|hideleg|hgeie|stvec|vstvec|mnepc|mncause|mnstatus).*[^W]ARL|warl_mask|write_mask.*(sip|sie|vsip|vsie|hip|hie|hvip|hedeleg|hideleg|hgeie|stvec|vstvec|mnepc|mncause|mnstatus)
