PR #2503  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: OpenSBI sets SSEED=1 in delegate_traps() to grant S-mode seed CSR access, but mseccfg is not saved/restored across non-retentive HSM suspend — after warm resume the SSEED/USEED bits revert to hardware reset values, breaking S-mode seed CSR access and violating the spec requirement that these bits have defined reset values the system must not leave undefined
Violation: During HSM non-retentive suspend/resume, OpenSBI saves and restores menvcfg, mie, mip, medeleg, mideleg but does NOT save/restore mseccfg. The SSEED/USEED bits configured by delegate_traps() during cold boot (sbi_hart.c:177-180) are lost after warm resume because delegate_traps() is not re-invoked on the warm-resume path. The hart resets to hardware-defined mseccfg reset values, which may differ from OpenSBI's intended runtime configuration.
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern: csr_set\(CSR_MSECCFG, MSECCFG_SSEED\)|csr_clear\(CSR_MSECCFG, MSECCFG_USEED\) but no corresponding CSR_MSECCFG save in __sbi_hsm_suspend_non_ret_save or restore in __sbi_hsm_suspend_non_ret_restore
