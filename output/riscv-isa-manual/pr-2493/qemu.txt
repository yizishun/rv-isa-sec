PR #2493  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Incorrect emulation of Smepmp stictky/lock-out bits in mseccfg and redefined pmpcfg.L semantics enables guest M-mode to bypass M-mode-only PMP enforcement, allowing privilege escalation from S/U-mode to M-mode memory access
Violation:
  QEMU TCG emulates mseccfg.MML or mseccfg.MMWP as non-sticky (writable to 0
  without PMP reset), allowing guest M-mode to clear these lockdown bits at
  runtime, or emulates mseccfg.RLB as settable while a locked PMP entry
  exists, violating the RLB lock-out semantics
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  mseccfg.*MML|mseccfg.*MMWP|mseccfg.*RLB|pmpcfg.*smepmp|mseccfg_write
