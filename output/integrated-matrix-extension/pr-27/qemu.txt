PR #27  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: IME spec now mandates round-to-odd for optional intermediate partial sum rounding (inner step), distinct from frm-based accumulation rounding (outer step); QEMU TCG must not reuse frm for both rounding stages or numerical results will be bit-incorrect vs. hardware
Violation: QEMU TCG implements IME floating-point matrix multiply-accumulate (e.g., vfmaccvv) and applies the CSR frm rounding mode to the intermediate partial sum S during multi-group accumulation, instead of round-to-odd (rto) as mandated: C_{m,n} ← round_frm(C_{m,n} + round_rto(S)). This causes the emulated result to diverge from hardware-visible behavior when G > 1 (multiple partial-sum groups) and the inner rto rounding is performed.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: ime.*round|float.*round.*mode.*frm|matrix.*accum|partial_sum.*round|riscv_frm|set_float_rounding_mode.*IME|trilinear.*rounding
