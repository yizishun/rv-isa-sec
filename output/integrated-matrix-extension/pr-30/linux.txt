PR #30  target=linux  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Unified VL divisibility to λ×LMUL across all GEMM instructions; kernel must ensure restored vtype/vl pairs satisfy the new invariant or risk illegal-instruction traps (DoS) from mismatched hardware/spec expectations on context switch boundaries.
Violation: Kernel restores vtype (with lambda, SEW, LMUL) and vl via sigreturn/ptrace/KVM without validating that vl is a multiple of (vtype.lambda × vtype.LMUL), allowing a userspace thread to observe hardware illegal-instruction traps or execute GEMM instructions with incoherent tile geometry depending on whether the hardware implements the old (inconsistent per-instruction) or new (unified λ×LMUL) VL divisibility checks.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: restore_v_state|__fpsimd_restore_state|riscv_vr_set|kvm_riscv_vcpu_set_reg|vtype.*LMUL.*vl|sigreturn.*v_ext
------------------------------------------------------------
[potential_risk] arch/riscv/kernel/signal.c:115-143  __restore_v_state
  sigreturn restores vtype and vl from userspace with zero validation. The function copies the entire __riscv_v_ext_state (including vstart, vtype, vl, vcsr, vlenb) directly from the user signal frame via __copy_from_user() at line 129-130 without checking vtype reserved bits, VLMAX consistency, or any vl-divisibility constraint. The hardware vsetvl in __vstate_csr_restore (vector.h:172) provides a safety net by setting vill for invalid vtype encodings and capping vl to VLMAX, but no kernel-side validation exists. When the proposed integrated-matrix λ field is added to vtype, this path will require a vl % (λ × LMUL) divisibility check.
