PR #3  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Intermediate rounding in matrix MAC pipeline must be decoupled from frm CSR; tying it to frm creates a software-controllable rounding-mode side-channel that leaks the hardware G-factor through numerically observable output differences when frm is toggled
Violation: In XiangShan RTL, the matrix multiply-accumulate datapath must partition the K_eff accumulation into groups of G sub-dot-products and round at group boundaries using an implementation-defined rounding mode that is independent of frm. A violation occurs if: (1) the intermediate rounding after each group of G sub-dot-products uses the frm CSR value instead of a hardwired implementation-defined rounding mode, making intermediate rounding behavior software-controllable when the spec mandates it be fixed per implementation; (2) the final accumulation step ignores frm and uses the implementation-defined mode instead; (3) the grouping factor G is not a power of two or falls outside [1, λ], causing either excessive precision loss (G too large, insufficient rounding) or excess rounding noise (G too small).
Consequence: function_loss
Pattern: matrix_mac rounding_mode_mux: intermediate_group_rounding_rm == frm (should be IMPLEMENTATION_DEFINED_ROUNDING) OR final_accumulation_rm != frm; G_compute: (lambda * LMUL) grouped by G where (G & (G-1)) != 0 OR G < 1 OR G > lambda
