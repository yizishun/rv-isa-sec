PR #694  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: iohgatp MODE=8 is overloaded (Sv32x4 when GXL=1, Sv39x4 when GXL=0); misinterpreting GXL causes G-stage walk with wrong levels/PTE-size, corrupting DMA target addresses
Violation: In QEMU, when iohgatp.MODE==8 and the differentiation via fctl.GXL is bypassed, stale, or TOCTOU-raced — causing MODE=8 to be interpreted as Sv32x4 under GXL=0 (wrong: 2 levels, 4-byte PTEs on a 64-bit guest) or as Sv39x4 under GXL=1 (wrong: 3 levels, 8-byte PTEs on a 32-bit guest) — the G-stage page table walk proceeds with incorrect level count and PTE size, producing a bogus SPA
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: switch on iohgatp.MODE where SV32X4==8 and SV39X4==8 share the same case label or are disambiguated solely by a separate GXL branch that is not atomic with the walk
