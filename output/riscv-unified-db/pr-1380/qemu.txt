PR #1380  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Smepmp redefines pmpcfg.L from 'locked' to 'M-mode-only' under MML and adds MMWP default-deny for M-mode; QEMU TCG PMP handlers must consult mseccfg fields on every access check or guest TEE/secure-boot isolation is silently broken
Violation:
  QEMU TCG performs PMP access checks without evaluating mseccfg.MML and
  mseccfg.MMWP bits, or mseccfg CSR write handler accepts MML/MMWP/RLB writes
  without triggering PMP behavior recalculation — causing the emulated PMP to
  enforce pre-Smepmp semantics (M-mode always passes, L=locked) when guest
  software has configured Smepmp containment (M-mode restricted, L=M-mode-
  only)
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  pmp_hart_has_privs|pmp_is_locked|mseccfg_write|mseccfg_csr.*write|case
  CSR_MSEECFG.*MML|MMWP.*mseccfg|pmpcfg.*L.*locked
