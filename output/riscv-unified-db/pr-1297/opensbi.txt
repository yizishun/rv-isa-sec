PR #1297  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: mseccfg exists iff Sm>=1.12 AND (Zkr|Smmpm|Zicfilp) is implemented; firmware must guard all mseccfg accesses with extension-specific checks, not Sm version alone
Violation:
  OpenSBI accesses mseccfg/mseccfgh CSR when the hart implements Sm >= 1.12
  but none of Zkr, Smmpm, or Zicfilp are implemented — the CSR does not exist
  at that privilege boundary and the access causes an unrecoverable M-mode
  illegal instruction trap
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern:
  csr_read(CSR_MSECCFG) or csr_write(CSR_MSECCFG) or csr_set(CSR_MSECCFG) or
  csr_clear(CSR_MSECCFG) without a surrounding extension guard
  (sbi_hart_has_extension SBI_HART_EXT_ZKR or SBI_HART_EXT_SMPMP or Zicfilp
  check); or equivalent direct CSR inline assembly on 0x747/0x757 guarded only
  by priv_version >= 1.12
