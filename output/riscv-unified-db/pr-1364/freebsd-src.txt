PR #1364  target=freebsd-src  overall=compliant
Spec: Ztso ISA extension silently ignored by identcpu.c parse_ext_z(); no elf_hwcap bit exposed; atomic.h, pmap.c, and busdma_bounce.c always emit full FENCE — correct under RVWMO but prevents Ztso-aware fence elision; future addition must ensure hart-homogeneous AND-reduction or risk data races on mixed-TSO/non-TSO systems
Violation:
  In the FreeBSD kernel, parse_ext_z() in sys/riscv/riscv/identcpu.c silently
  ignores the 'ztso' extension when parsing the FDT 'riscv,isa' property — no
  Z_ZTso flag exists in the z_extensions bitfield, no has_ztso global is
  updated, and elf_hwcap receives no Ztso bit for AT_HWCAP. Consequently,
  atomic.h unconditionally emits full 'fence iorw,iorw' in every primitive
  (acquire, release, seq_cst, mb/rmb/wmb), and pmap.c always inserts a D-Cache
  FENCE before every sfence.vma in TLB shootdown paths. On Ztso hardware this
  is over-fenced but correct; however, if Ztso detection is later added and
  used to elide fences (the whole motivation for Ztso), the AND-reduction in
  update_global_capabilities() — which correctly requires all harts to share a
  capability — could be bypassed by a naive patch that checks only the BSP's
  ISA string, eliding fences on non-TSO secondary harts.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern:
  CHECK_Z_EXT\((?!.*ztso).*\)|z_extensions.*<<|has_ztso|fence\(\)|sfence\.vma|
  atomic.*_acq_.*fence|atomic.*_rel_.*fence
