PR #1009  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Incomplete multi-level SSE enable-check in Zicfiss instruction emulation bypasses supervisor's ability to disable shadow stack for U-mode or virtualized guests
Violation:
  QEMU TCG helper for Zicfiss instructions checks only CSR[menvcfg].SSE but
  neglects CSR[senvcfg].SSE for U-mode and CSR[henvcfg].SSE for VS/VU-mode,
  allowing shadow-stack operations at privilege levels where the controlling
  envcfg has SSE=0
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  helper_sspush|helper_sspopchk|helper_ssrdp|helper_ssamoswap
