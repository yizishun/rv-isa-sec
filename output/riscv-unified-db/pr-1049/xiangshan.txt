PR #1049  target=xiangshan  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Zvfofp8min introduces FP8 OFP8 (E4M3/E5M2) convert ops at SEW=8; XiangShan VFPU must correctly decode new funct6 encodings, nan-box 8-bit results in VF registers, implement saturating narrowing clamp (not truncation) for FP32→OFP8, and validate VLEN≥32 when VSEW=8 against Zve32f constraint
Violation:
  XiangShan VFPU decode incorrectly matches Zvfofp8min OFP8 convert
  instruction encodings against existing vector ALU opcodes, or implements
  narrowing FP32→OFP8 saturation with wraparound instead of saturation, or
  fails to nan-box OFP8 results in vector destination registers, or computes
  VL/SEW for OFP8 (SEW=8) without validating VSETVL constraints against Zve32f
  minimum VLEN, or executes OFP8 convert ops when mstatus.VS=Off without
  raising illegal-instruction
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern:
  VFPU decoder match for OFP8 convert funct6/funct3 opcode fields; narrowing
  conversion saturation mux using truncation instead of clamp; VF register
  writeback without upper-bit nan-boxing when SEW=8; VSETVL SEW=8 path
  skipping VLEN≥32 check; FP8 E4M3/E5M2 format conversion LUT with incorrect
  NaN/denormal encoding
