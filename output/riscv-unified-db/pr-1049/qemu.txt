PR #1049  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Zvfofp8min adds OFP8↔BF16/FP32 vector conversions; QEMU must correctly emulate E4M3/E5M2 softfloat encode/decode, saturating vs non-saturating quad-narrowing element layout, and float_status flags to avoid silent corruption of guest vector register data in TCG.
Violation:
  QEMU TCG incorrectly translates Zvfofp8min conversion instructions (e.g.,
  vfncvtbf16.f.f.w with OFP8 dest, vfwcvtf.f.f.v with OFP8 source,
  vfncvtfp32bf16.f.f.w quad-narrowing) such that: (1) the E4M3/E5M2 format
  decode/encode is wrong (wrong exponent bias, wrong mantissa width, wrong
  NaN/infinity/subnormal handling), (2) saturation in narrowing conversions is
  skipped or incorrectly clamped, (3) the 4:1 quad-narrowing element packing
  mixes up destination register lanes, or (4) TCG flags (float_status,
  rounding mode, exception flags) are not correctly configured for the
  narrower OFP8 types.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern:
  (opivv|opfv|gen_helper).*ofp8|float_to_ofp8|ofp8_to_float|e4m3|e5m2|vnclip.*
  ofp|vfwcvt.*ofp|vfncvt.*ofp8|quad_narrow.*fp32|softfloat.*ofp8|set_fp_excp.*
  ofp8
