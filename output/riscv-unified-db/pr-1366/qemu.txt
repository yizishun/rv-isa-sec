PR #1366  target=qemu  overall=not_implemented
Spec: Ssu32xl requires sstatus.UXL writable to 1 (UXLEN=32); QEMU must accept UXL=1 writes, flush TCG TBs on UXL change, and correctly restrict U-mode instruction decode, GPR width, and memory addressing to 32 bits — failure to do so lets a 32-bit guest U-mode process execute 64-bit instructions or leak upper register halves, crossing the architectural privilege boundary from U-mode into 64-bit supervisor-visible state.
Violation:
  QEMU advertises Ssu32xl support (e.g., via misa or device tree) but
  hardwires sstatus.UXL to 2 (UXLEN=64), OR accepts UXL=1 writes but TCG
  continues to decode and execute U-mode instructions as 64-bit (e.g.,
  translate.c does not consult get_ol(ctx) or equivalent when selecting insn32
  vs insn64 decode paths, or gen_load/goto_tb do not truncate GPR writes to 32
  bits in U-mode), OR a CSR write to sstatus.UXL does not trigger a TB flush
  causing stale translations to execute at the wrong XLEN, OR KVM passes
  through a UXL=1 write without validating host capability causing host-side
  misbehavior.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern:
  sstatus_write.*uxl|set_uxl|get_xl.*ol|get_ol|has_ext.*Ssu32xl|uxl.*warl|warl
  .*uxl|translate.*xl|gen_goto_tb.*xl|truncate.*xlen|sign_extend.*xl|tb_flush.
  *uxl|sstatus.*mask.*0x[0-9a-f]*30000
