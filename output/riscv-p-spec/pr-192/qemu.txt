PR #192  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Encoding collision between PMULHR.W and MULHR (both funct2=0x3, funct4=0x0 in OP-32) creates ambiguous decode; PMULH.W/PMULHR.W require RV64-only guards; TCG translation must correctly extract upper-half of double-width intermediate products with proper signed/unsigned zero-extension and rounding-constant width matching XLEN
Violation: In QEMU TCG, if the decoder disambiguates PMULHR.W and MULHR incorrectly due to their identical encoding (both OP-32, funct2=0x3, funct4=0x0, bit[25]=0x1), or if PMULH.W/PMULHR.W are not gated behind RV64-only checks (causing illegal-op on RV32 to be missed or wrong op on RV64), guest code executing one instruction gets the other instruction's semantics — producing silently wrong packed vs. scalar multiply-high results in the destination register.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: trans_pmulh\.c|decode_op32.*funct2.*0x3|riscv_has_ext.*zpnh|tcg_gen_mul_i64.*extract|RV64.*guard.*PMULH\.W
