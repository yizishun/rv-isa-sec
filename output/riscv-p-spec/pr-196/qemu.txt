PR #196  target=qemu  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Accumulating packed SIMD multiply-add instructions (PM4ADDA.B/PM4ADDASU.B/PM4ADDAU.B/PM4ADDA.H) require correct signed/unsigned extension per instruction variant; a sign-extension mismatch on product terms causes silently wrong guest results indistinguishable from guest software bugs
Violation: QEMU TCG incorrectly applies sign-extension (sext32) instead of zero-extension (zext32) to the per-product terms in PM4ADDAU.B, or applies zext32 instead of sext32 to PM4ADDA.B/PM4ADDASU.B. TCG also violates the spec if it swaps the signed/unsigned operand roles in PM4ADDASU.B (rs1 must be signed, rs2 must be unsigned) or if it processes the wrong number of 32-bit lanes (e.g., iterating once instead of XLEN/32 times on RV64).
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: tcg_gen_(ext|add|mul).*PM4|gen_pm4.*ext|PM4ADD.*helper|signed.*unsigned.*swap.*PM4|zext32.*sext32.*PM4|OP-32.*P-ext.*decode
------------------------------------------------------------

  get_gpr() applies DisasExtend (EXT_SIGN/EXT_ZERO/EXT_NONE) to the WHOLE register, not per 32-bit lane.
