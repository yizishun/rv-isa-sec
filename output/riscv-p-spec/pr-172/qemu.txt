PR #172  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Double-wide register-pair P-add instructions encode register-pair selectors as 4-bit fields but require even-numbered registers; failing to validate this in QEMU's decode/translate causes aliased register-pair writes that silently corrupt guest register state
Violation: QEMU TCG decoder for PADD.DB/DH/DW/DBS/DHS/DWS (OP-IMM-32 major opcode 0x1b, funct3=0x6) does not enforce that the register-pair operands rd_p, rs1_p, rs2_p (encoded as 4-bit fields) specify even-numbered registers. An odd value in these fields causes register aliasing where X[odd*2] and X[odd*2+1] overlap with adjacent pairs — e.g., rd_p=1 writes to X[2] and X[3], but X[2] is also the high half of rd_p=0's pair, producing silent cross-instruction data corruption.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: trans_ppair | gen_helper_padd_db | OP_IMM_32.*0x6 | ext4.*rd_p | register_pair.*align | GEN_HANDLER.*PADD\.DW | decodetree.*OP-IMM-32.*funct3=6
------------------------------------------------------------
