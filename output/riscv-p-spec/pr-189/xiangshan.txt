PR #189  target=xiangshan  overall=potential_risk
Spec: P-ext REV (full-XLEN bit-reverse) has no decoder entry and no ALU datapath in XiangShan; RV32 (funct12=0x69f) and RV64 (funct12=0x6bf) encodings differ but no XLEN-aware decode logic exists, risking illegal-exception DoS or silent wrong-result execution.
Violation: XiangShan decoder has no entry for REV (funct12=0x69f RV32 / 0x6bf RV64 under OP-IMM+funct3=0x5). The existing ALU datapath only supports byte-level bit-reverse (revb/BREV8 at ALUOpType.revb) and byte-level byte-swap (rev8/REV8 at ALUOpType.rev8), but has no full-XLEN bit-reverse operation. When the decoder encounters these funct12 values, either an illegal-instruction exception fires where it should not (DoS for P-ext software) or the pattern collides with a different instruction due to undriven decode defaults, silently producing wrong results. Additionally, no XLEN-aware encoding selection logic exists to distinguish the RV32-only funct12 (0x69f) from the RV64-only funct12 (0x6bf), so a misconfigured or incorrectly-constrained Chisel decode table could map both to the same (wrong) micro-op.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: funct12 == 0x69f || funct12 == 0x6bf || REV || full bit reverse XLEN || Cat(Reverse(src1))
