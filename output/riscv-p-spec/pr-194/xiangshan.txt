PR #194  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Three encoding collisions between MUL.Hxx and PMUL.W.Hxx pairs (identical opcode/funct3/funct2/funct4, only distinguishable by XLEN context) create instruction decode ambiguity on RV64 that silently corrupts packed SIMD multiply results if decoder selects wrong semantic variant
Violation: Instruction decoder maps identical 32-bit encoding (OP-32=0x3b, funct3 identical, funct2=0x1, funct4 identical) to the wrong operation on RV64. MUL.H00 and PMUL.W.H00 share funct3=0x3/funct4=0x0; MUL.H01 and PMUL.W.H01 share funct3=0x1/funct4=0x2; MUL.H11 and PMUL.W.H11 share funct3=0x3/funct4=0x2. On RV64, the decoder cannot statically distinguish these three instruction pairs by opcode+funct bits alone, yet the spec defines semantically distinct operations: MUL.Hxx computes a single XLEN-wide signed product from one halfword pair with sign-extension, while PMUL.W.Hxx computes two independent packed 32-bit products from two halfword pairs across word lanes. If XiangShan decodes the encoding to MUL.Hxx while software expects PMUL.W.Hxx (or vice versa), the ALU multiplier datapath feeds wrong source operands and writes wrong dest lane results.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: MUL_H00.*PMUL_W_H00|funct3.*0x[13].*funct4.*0x[02].*funct2.*0x1|P-ext.*cross.*element.*decode.*collision|XLEN.*dependent.*OP-32.*decode
