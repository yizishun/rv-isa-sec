PR #727  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: Smcsps stack-swap instructions now raise illegal-instruction when x2[3:0] != 0, requiring the kernel to guarantee 128B alignment of all stack pointers touched across context-switch, signal-delivery, and KVM vCPU restore paths; any violation triggers an unexpected trap that crashes the kernel or guest.
Violation: The kernel (or KVM host on behalf of a guest) writes a value to x2, msp.SP, or ssp.SP whose lower 4 bits are non-zero, and subsequently the hart executes c.mspush, c.mspop, c.sspush, or c.sspop — either directly in the kernel context-switch path, during signal return, or when resuming a vCPU whose stack pointer was corrupted by the host.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: stores to ss/csr 0x300 (msp), 0x600 (ssp), or x2 without masking lower 4 bits (e.g. missing & ~0xf); arithmetic on saved stack pointers in switch_to, __switch_to, __fstate_save, __fstate_restore, or KVM's mvendorid.Smcsp handling without realignment before mret/sret/VM-entry; signal frame setup (setup_sigframe, setup_rt_frame) computing sp without enforcing 128B alignment when Smcsps is active
