PR #785  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: IMSIC eip/eie registers manage pending/enable bits in local eistate[] without cross-referencing APLIC sourcecfg SM_INACTIVE status, violating spec requirement that inactive-source bits are read-only zero
Violation: QEMU's IMSIC (riscv_imsic.c) manages eip/eie bits independently in eistate[] via riscv_imsic_eix_rmw(), without checking whether the source is inactive (SM == INACTIVE or delegated) in the connected APLIC domain. The spec requires that eip and eie bits for inactive sources must be read-only zero, but QEMU's IMSIC reads/writes these bits from purely local state. The only cross-check is the hardcoded bit0 of eip0/eie0 read-only-zero rule at riscv_imsic.c:176-178.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: riscv_imsic_eix_rmw reading eistate without cross-checking aplic->sourcecfg[irq] SM field for inactive/delegated status; missing riscv_aplic_source_active() call when aliasing eip/eie to APLIC domain
