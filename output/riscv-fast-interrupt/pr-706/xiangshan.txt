PR #706  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Unratified MIPU/SIPU control bits (no allocated CSR encoding) gate automatic interrupt stacking at M/S-mode trap entry — incorrect bit position, missing/polarity-inverted gating MUX, or stale CSR sampling in trap-entry hardware causes privilege-level trap-flow divergence and potential state corruption on mret/sret
Violation: In XiangShan RTL, when MIPU (mstatus bit) or SIPU (sstatus bit) gates automatic interrupt stacking at M-mode or S-mode trap entry respectively. A violation occurs under any of: (1) the CSR bit-position encoding for MIPU/SIPU in mstatus/sstatus is hardcoded before ratification — the bits are explicitly unallocated, so any chosen offset may collide with other ratified mstatus fields (e.g., SD, TSR, TW, TVM, MXR, SUM, MPRV, XS, FS, VS, UBE, SBE, MBE) or future extensions, causing the stacking feature to be gate-controlled by an unrelated field; (2) TrapEntryMEventModule/TrapEntryHSEventModule unconditionally perform automatic stacking (or unconditionally skip it) without reading the MIPU/SIPU bit value at trap-entry time — i.e., the gating MUX is missing; (3) the polarity of the bit is inverted relative to the spec (bit=0 enables stacking instead of bit=1); (4) M-mode trap entry reads SIPU instead of MIPU or S-mode reads MIPU instead of SIPU (cross-privilege gating); (5) the stacking enable is sampled from the CSR at the wrong pipeline stage (e.g., stale mstatus value before the write that cleared MIPU has taken effect), creating a TOCTOU window where a hart that disabled stacking still undergoes automatic save/restore.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: TrapEntry(M|HS)Event.*MIPU|TrapEntry(M|HS)Event.*SIPU|automatic.?stack.*enable|mstatus.*MIPU|sstatus.*SIPU|auto.?save.*interrupt|stacking.*gate|MIPU.*WARL|SIPU.*WARL
