PR #706  target=xiangshan  overall=compliant
Spec: Unallocated MIPU/SIPU control bits in mstatus/sstatus gate automatic interrupt stacking at M/S level; premature hardcoding of bit positions in RTL creates encoding collision risk, and incorrect gating of mipopret/sipopret on these bits violates spec-mandated independence
Violation: MIPU/SIPU bits are placed at unallocated encoding positions in mstatus/sstatus (spec explicitly states 'These bits do not yet have an allocated encoding'); if XiangShan hardcodes a provisional bit position in the CSR register file, any future ratified encoding change will cause a bit-level mismatch. Additionally, if the hardware implementation gates automatic stacking on these bits but fails to ensure that mipopret/sipopret behavior is independent of their state (as required by the second NOTE), incorrect prestacking/restoration occurs on privilege returns.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: mstatus.*MIPU|sstatus.*SIPU|automatic.*stacking.*gate|mipopret.*MIPU|sipopret.*SIPU|CLIC.*trap.*stacking
