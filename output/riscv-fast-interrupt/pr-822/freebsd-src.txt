PR #822  target=freebsd-src  overall=compliant
Spec: ACLIC reuses IMSIC CSRs (stopei/eip/eie) but gates them behind mstateen0 bit 58; FreeBSD kernel has no Smstateen awareness and would trap on access if M-mode firmware disables the bit, causing kernel panic
Violation: FreeBSD kernel (S-mode) on a system implementing ACLIC accesses stopei, eip[k], or eie[k] CSRs while the controlling mstateen0 CSR has bit 58 clear. M-mode firmware (e.g., OpenSBI) may clear mstateen0[58] at boot or after a warm reset, and the kernel has no mechanism to detect this before touching the IMSIC-reused CSRs, resulting in an unexpected illegal instruction trap.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: csr_read(stopei) OR csr_write(stopei, .*) OR csr_read(eip) OR csr_write(eip, .*) OR csr_read(eie) OR csr_write(eie, .*) without preceding mstateen0 bit-58 check
