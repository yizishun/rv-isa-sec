PR #822  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: OpenSBI domain context switch must preserve mstateen0 bit 58 (IMSIC) even when IMSIC is not present, or S-mode loses access to ACLIC-reused stopei/eip/eie CSRs after resume
Violation: When OpenSBI performs domain context save/restore (e.g., HSM suspend/resume) and handles mstateen0 without preserving bit 58 (IMSIC), either by (a) explicitly writing a subset of bits and zeroing bit 58, or (b) failing to save/restore mstateen0 entirely while the caller depends on bit 58 being set to grant S-mode access to stopei/eip[e]/eie[e] CSRs reused by ACLIC
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: mstateen0 CSR write with a mask that omits bit 58, or domain context CSR save/restore list missing mstateen0 when ACLIC-stopei/eip/eie CSRs are also saved/restored
