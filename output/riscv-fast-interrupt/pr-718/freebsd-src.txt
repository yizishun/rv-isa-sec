PR #718  target=freebsd-src  overall=compliant
Spec: sithreshold on trap entry changed from per-interrupt priority (iprio[i]) to level-max priority (IPRIO), blocking all same-level nested interrupts that FreeBSD may rely on
Violation: FreeBSD kernel interrupt handlers or scheduler assume same-level interrupt nesting (old iprio[i] threshold permitting higher-priority interrupts within a level to preempt), but hardware following the new spec sets sithreshold to IPRIO (the level's maximum priority), silently blocking all same-level nesting and starving time-critical handlers.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: sithreshold|sipstatus|nested.*interrupt|INTR_PRIORITY|intr_priority|riscv_intr.*mask|setirqmask|CLINT.*threshold
