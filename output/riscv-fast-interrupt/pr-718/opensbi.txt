PR #718  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: Bug: mithreshold set to iprio[i] instead of IPRIO on M-mode interrupt entry — higher-priority interrupts can preempt OpenSBI M-mode handlers, causing re-entrant corruption of HSM context, PMP config, or domain-context save/restore state.
Violation: In OpenSBI M-mode, when an interrupt i is taken, the spec bug sets mithreshold ← iprio[i] instead of mithreshold ← IPRIO (max priority, all-1s), allowing any higher-priority interrupt (iprio[j] > iprio[i]) to preempt the in-progress M-mode trap handler before it can save critical context or complete its atomic section.
Consequence: race_condition
Pattern: CSR read/write of mithreshold or mintthresh without surrounding interrupt-disable fencing; M-mode trap handlers lacking mstatus.MIE=0 or mithreshold=IPRIO as the first instruction; nested M-mode handler paths that assume non-reentrancy of PMP, domain-context, or HSM save/restore sequences.
