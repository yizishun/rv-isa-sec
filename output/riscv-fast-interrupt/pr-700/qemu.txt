PR #700  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: SMIP reorders cspspush/cspspop relative to register save/restore in trap entry/exit; QEMU TCG using the old ordering corrupts handler stack on entry and leaks interrupted-context state on exit
Violation: QEMU TCG implements the SMIP trap entry/exit sequence with the pre-PR ordering (cspspush/cspspop at the old positions). On trap entry, saving x10-x15 after cspspush means registers land on the handler stack instead of the interrupted context's stack, corrupting the handler stack. On trap exit, restoring x10-x15 before cspspop means registers are loaded from the handler stack instead of the interrupted context's stack, leaking or corrupting context state.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: cspspush.*save.*x1[0-5]|save.*adjust.*cspspush|cspspop.*restore.*x1[0-5]|lr.*OF.*cspspop|cspspop.*adjust.*stack
