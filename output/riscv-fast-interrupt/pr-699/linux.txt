PR #699  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: xivt.EHV bit silently redirects synchronous exceptions (ECALLs, page faults, illegal instructions) through the xivt-based vector table instead of xtvec; kernel context switch and KVM must atomically save/restore xivt.EHV alongside xivt to prevent a guest or userspace task from hijacking the kernel's trap entry path.
Violation: In the Linux kernel, when a context switch or VM-entry/VM-exit saves and restores xivt (sivt/mivt/hivt) without also saving and restoring the xivt.EHV bit, or when the kernel assumes synchronous exceptions always trap via xtvec while xivt.EHV is set, the kernel's trap handler entry logic is bypassed or corrupted.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: (sivt|mivt|hivt).*(save|restore|swap|switch)|CSR_(SIVT|MIVT|HIVT).*(read|write|swap)|ehv|xIVT.*EHV|smehv.*save|ssehv.*context
