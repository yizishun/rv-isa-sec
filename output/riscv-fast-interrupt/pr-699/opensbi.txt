PR #699  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: Unchecked EHV bit in mivt CSR allows misconfigured synchronous exception vectoring that can redirect M-mode traps to attacker-controlled addresses, breaking the M-mode trust boundary
Violation: OpenSBI writes to mivt CSR (0x307) to configure the M-mode trap vector base without explicitly clearing or setting the EHV bit, OR writes to mivt without a subsequent fence instruction, causing the synchronous exception hardware vectoring behavior to take effect on a stale or attacker-controlled trap vector configuration.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: csrw\s+mivt|csrs\s+mivt|csrc\s+mivt|write_csr\(CSR_MIVT|SBI_CSR_SET.*0x307|li.*0x307.*csrw
