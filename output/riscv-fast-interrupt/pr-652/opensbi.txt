PR #652  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: Smaiae removal means OpenSBI must handle full Smaia virtual interrupt CSRs (mvip/mvien) actively rather than relying on hardware read-only zero — uninitialized mvien allows S-mode to inject interrupts via mvip, escaping M-mode interrupt delivery control.
Violation: OpenSBI M-mode code assumes Smaiae semantics (mvien/mvip read-only zero, no virtual interrupt filtering) and does not sanitize mvien to read-only zero before delegating interrupts or entering S-mode, but the underlying hart implements full Smaia where mvien and mvip are writable and virtual interrupts are active.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: OpenSBI writes to mvip or mvien, or OpenSBI assumes mvip/mvien are read-only zero and skips initialization/clearing of virtual interrupt state before entering S-mode on Smaia hardware.
