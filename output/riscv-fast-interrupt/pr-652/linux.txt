PR #652  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: Smaiae removal lifts the read-only-zero guarantee on virtual interrupt CSRs (mvip/mvien); kernel must now context-switch and initialize these CSRs to prevent stale interrupt masks from silently dropping or reordering interrupts on Smaia hardware
Violation: Kernel interrupt controller code (e.g., AIA/ACLIC driver, KVM IRQ injection path) saves/restores or initializes mvip/mvien CSRs under the assumption they are read-only zero (the Smaiae guarantee), but the hardware now implements full Smaia where these virtual interrupt pending/enable registers are writable and functional, causing stale or uninitialized interrupt filtering state
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: mvip|mvien|hvip|hvien|CSR_MVIP|CSR_MVIEN|smaiae|SMAIAE|virtual_interrupt|is_smaiae
