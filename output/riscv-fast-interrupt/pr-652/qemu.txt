PR #652  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: PR removes Smaiae (which forced xvip/xvien read-only zero) and promotes ACLIC dependency to full Smaia/Ssaia; QEMU must ensure future ACLIC CSR implementations validate ext_smaia/ext_ssaia with functional mvip/mvien, not the removed Smaiae subset.
Violation: If QEMU implements ACLIC extensions (Smaclic/Ssaclic/Smnip/Ssnip/Smehv) in the future but gates them on a hypothetical ext_smaiae flag that forces mvip/mvien read-only zero — as the old spec permitted — instead of requiring full ext_smaia/ext_ssaia with functional virtual interrupt CSRs, guest software relying on interrupt filtering through mvip/mvien will receive no interrupts on bits 1, 5, and 9, violating the updated dependency chain.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: ext_smaiae || Smaiae || smaiae — any ACLIC CSR registration (smclic/ssclic/smnip/ssnip/smehv) gated on a removed/non-existent Smaiae flag instead of the full Smaia/Ssaia flag, or mvip_writable_mask/mvien_writable_mask zeroed out when ACLIC features are active.
