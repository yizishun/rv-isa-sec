PR #729  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: IVT mode trap delivery must auto-clear the serviced interrupt's pending bit upon successful handler-address fetch; missing this side-effect causes the interrupt to repeatedly re-fire, effectively hanging the guest in an interrupt storm (DoS).
Violation: When QEMU delivers an interrupt via CLIC IVT (Interrupt Vector Table) mode, the emulation reads the handler address from VTBASE but does NOT clear the corresponding interrupt-pending bit (clicintip[i]) for the serviced interrupt before setting PC, OR unconditionally clears it for level-sensitive interrupts where the spec mandates 'if possible' semantics, meaning level-sensitive interrupts may remain pending due to the external source still asserting the level.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: ivt_fetch.*handler|vtbase.*fetch|riscv_cpu_do_interrupt.*clic|clint.*vectored.*clear|clic_intip.*clear|vt_entry.*pc_set|clear.*pending.*ivt
