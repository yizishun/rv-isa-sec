PR #669  target=freebsd-src  overall=compliant
Spec: Removal of eithreshold/eidelivery from Smidctrl/Ssidctrl scope breaks FreeBSD's interrupt-threshold gating on ACLIC-only systems, opening critical-section race windows when the kernel expects threshold-based masking but the CSR is absent or repurposed as mithreshold with different (major+minor identity) semantics.
Violation: FreeBSD kernel executes CSR read/write to eithreshold or eidelivery on a hart implementing Smidctrl/Ssidctrl but not full IMSIC, where these CSRs are no longer architecturally present per the revised spec, causing either an illegal-instruction trap or (if the access is caught and emulated) undefined interrupt-priority behavior during critical sections such as spinlock-held regions or context-switch windows.
Consequence: race_condition
