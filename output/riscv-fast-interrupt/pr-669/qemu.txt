PR #669  target=qemu  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Smidctrl/Ssidctrl removes eidelivery and eithreshold from scope and adds mithreshold; stale ISELECT access to eidelivery/eithreshold while mithreshold is absent enables guest interrupt-delivery manipulation outside the spec-defined Smidctrl control model
Violation: When QEMU enables ext_smidctrl or ext_ssidctrl but the indirect CSR access path (sireg*/vsireg* via ISELECT windowing) still permits read/write access to ISELECT_IMSIC_EIDELIVERY (0x70) and ISELECT_IMSIC_EITHRESHOLD (0x72), which the updated spec explicitly removes from Smidctrl/Ssidctrl scope. Additionally, the new mithreshold CSR (IPRIOLEN bits, applies to major+minor interrupt identities) is absent while the stale eithreshold path remains reachable.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: ISELECT-based indirect CSR access (sireg*/vsireg* read/write handlers) gate on ext_ssaia but lack ext_smidctrl/ssidctrl checks to block eidelivery (ISELECT 0x70) and eithreshold (ISELECT 0x72); missing mithreshold CSR registration alongside reachable old eithreshold path

[potential_risk] target/riscv/csr.c:6100-6210  
  CSR registration table has no entries for mithreshold (CSR 0x347) or sithreshold (CSR 0x147). These CSRs are defined by the ACLIC spec as new WLRL CSRs implementing IPRIOLEN bits that apply the priority threshold to both major and minor interrupt identities. When Smidctrl/Ssidctrl are implemented, these must be registered. Their absence alongside reachable eithreshold would mean the guest uses the wrong threshold mechanism.
