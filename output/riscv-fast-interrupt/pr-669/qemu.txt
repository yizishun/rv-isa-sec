PR #669  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Stale eidelivery/eithreshold emulation after spec removes them from Smidctrl/Ssidctrl scope blocks or corrupts guest interrupt delivery when guest targets the updated spec
Violation: QEMU emulates Smidctrl/Ssidctrl but fails to remove the WARL-forced eidelivery=0x20000000 behavior and continues to expose eithreshold as subsuming APLIC ithreshold. Guest software built against the updated spec expects eidelivery and eithreshold to be outside Smidctrl scope (i.e., behave per base AIA or be absent), and instead finds eidelivery hardwired to ACLIC-only mode with eithreshold aliasing APLIC state, breaking the guest's interrupt delivery model.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: smuidctrl OR ssidctrl flag check that gates eidelivery WARL 0x20000000 or eithreshold-to-ithreshold aliasing; any CSR read/write handler for eidelivery/eithreshold that branches on smstateen/mstateen Smidctrl/Ssidctrl bits; missing mithreshold CSR when Smidctrl/Ssidctrl is enabled
