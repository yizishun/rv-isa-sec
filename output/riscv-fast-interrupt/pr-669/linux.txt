PR #669  target=linux  overall=compliant
Spec: Removal of eithreshold from Smidctrl/Ssidctrl scope means kernel code touching eithreshold CSR on these systems encounters undefined CSR access, risking illegal-instruction traps; kernel must migrate to mithreshold which differs in applying thresholds to both major and minor interrupt identities.
Violation: Linux kernel accesses the eithreshold CSR on a hart implementing Smidctrl or Ssidctrl, where eithreshold is no longer included in scope and its interrupt-threshold function is replaced by the new mithreshold CSR with different semantics (applies to both major and minor interrupt identities, not just major).
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: csr_read.*(0x352|CSR_EITHRESHOLD|eithreshold)|csr_write.*(0x352|CSR_EITHRESHOLD|eithreshold)|CSR_EITHRESHOLD
