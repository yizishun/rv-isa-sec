PR #732  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: CLIC trap entry must distinguish interrupts from exceptions: only interrupts raise xithreshold to IPRIO; exceptions save but don't modify threshold; double-traps in M-mode must preserve original pithreshold — confusing these cases causes incorrect interrupt masking on xRET, guest privilege escalation via unintended threshold lowering
Violation: In QEMU's CLIC trap entry (riscv_cpu_do_interrupt or equivalent), if the handler (1) sets xithreshold = IPRIO on synchronous exceptions instead of only on async interrupts, or (2) fails to save xithreshold to xpistatus.pithreshold on exception entry, or (3) overwrites xpistatus.pithreshold on a double-trap while already in M-mode (env->priv == PRV_M)
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: riscv_cpu_do_interrupt.*xithreshold|async.*xithreshold|xithreshold.*async|pithreshold.*overwrite|double.trap.*pithreshold|env->priv.*PRV_M.*xithreshold|xithreshold\s*=\s*[^I]
------------------------------------------------------------
