PR #775  target=freebsd-src  overall=potential_risk
Spec: Kernel that writes to aclicsourcecfg[0] or target[0] iprio fields believing it configures interrupt #0 will silently get ROZ behavior, leaving IRQ #0 effectively dead/unroutable without the kernel realizing the misconfiguration.
Violation: FreeBSD's APLIC driver writes to aclicsourcecfg[0] via xiselect=0x1000/xireg2 (XLEN=32) or acliciprio[0] to configure interrupt source #0 or target #0, and subsequently assumes the write succeeded — but the hardware silently drops the write because sourcecfg[0] and target[0] are hardwired read-only zero across all APLIC domains.
Consequence: denial_of_service
Pattern: for.*(i\s*=\s*0|int\s+i\s*=\s*0).*sourcecfg\[|for.*(i\s*=\s*0).*target\[|xiselect.*=.*0x1000.*xireg2
