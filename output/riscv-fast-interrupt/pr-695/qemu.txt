PR #695  target=qemu  overall=compliant
Spec: Pre-fix mithreshold CSR at 0xFB1 lands in M-mode debug trigger CSR address space (0x7A0-0x7BF); a guest write to mithreshold at 0xFB1 would alias to debug trigger registers, allowing unprivileged interrupt-priority manipulation to corrupt M-mode debug state or vice versa — a CSR address collision enabling cross-privilege state corruption.
Violation: QEMU implements the Smnip CSRs at the old (incorrect) CSR addresses from the pre-fix spec, specifically mithreshold at 0xFB1 (which overlaps the debug trigger CSR address range 0x7A0-0x7BF) and sithreshold at 0x1B1 (non-standard offset), instead of the corrected contiguous block at M-mode 0x346-0x349 / S-mode 0x146-0x149.
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: gen_helper_wr_mithreshold|csr_ops\[0xFB1\]|csr_ops\[0x1B1\]|"mithreshold".*0xFB1|"sithreshold".*0x1B1|csr_number.*0xFB1|csr_number.*0x1B1
