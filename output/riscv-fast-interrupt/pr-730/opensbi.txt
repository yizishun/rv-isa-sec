PR #730  target=opensbi  overall=bug_confirmed
Spec: M-mode interrupt save/restore must complete register saves before sp adjustment and guard on double-trap (Smrnmi for M-mode) availability, or unrecoverable faults will corrupt trap state in OpenSBI.
Violation: OpenSBI M-mode interrupt handler executes Smip/Smaia context save/restore (stack pointer swap, selective register save) on hardware lacking the double-trap or Smrnmi extension, OR saves/restores registers after adjusting the stack pointer—making synchronous exceptions during trap side effects unrecoverable.
Consequence: data_corruption
Pattern: (mnepc|mepc|mstatus\.mpp|mscratch|sscratch|sp\s*<-|sp\s*=).*save|restore|swap|csrw.*mstatus|csrw.*mepc
------------------------------------------------------------
