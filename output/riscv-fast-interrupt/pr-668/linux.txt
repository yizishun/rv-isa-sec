PR #668  target=linux  overall=potential_risk
Spec: If KVM or kernel interrupt handling reads xtopei.IPRIO and uses it as a raw priority value rather than a threshold-mapped value, major interrupts with iprio[i] > 255 all collapse to IPRIO=255, causing incorrect priority arbitration and potential guest VM privilege escalation through interrupt-priority injection
Violation: Kernel reads xtopei.IPRIO and assumes it equals the raw interrupt source priority iprio[i], then uses it for software-based priority arbitration or exposes it to a guest VM without accounting for: (1) saturation to 255 for iprio[i] > 255, and (2) priority=0 mapping to either 0 or 255 depending on whether the default priority is higher or lower than a machine external interrupt
Consequence: privilege_escape
Pattern: csr_read.*xtopei|xTOPEI|topi.*prio|IPRIO.*xithreshold|sxtopei.*prio|mtopei.*prio
