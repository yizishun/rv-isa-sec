PR #72  target=qemu  overall=not_implemented
Spec: CSR privilege demotion (SRW/HRW→URW) or missing V=1 substitution for sacecrstatus→vsacecrstatus would let guest user-mode read per-CR dirty/clean state, leaking cryptographic context metadata across privilege boundaries.
Violation:
  QEMU fails to redirect sacecrstatus CSR accesses to vsacecrstatus when V=1
  in the hypervisor extension, or exposes sacecrstatus/vsacecrstatus with the
  old unprivileged (URW/URO) privilege level instead of the new SRW/HRW
  levels, or does not raise an illegal-instruction exception when an ACE
  instruction targets a CR whose per-CR 2-bit field in
  sacecrstatus/vsacecrstatus is Off (value 0).
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern:
  sacecrstatus|vsacecrstatus|acecrstatus.*csr_ops|ace.*crstatus.*read|ace.*crs
  tatus.*write|riscv_csrrw.*acecrstatus
------------------------------------------------------------
