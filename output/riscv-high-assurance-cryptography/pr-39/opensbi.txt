PR #39  target=opensbi  overall=compliant
Spec: Writing mstatus.ACES=Initial/Off does NOT clear ACE CC hardware state; cryptographic context leaks across hart suspend/resume or domain switches unless ace.reset is explicitly issued first
Violation: OpenSBI writes mstatus.ACES=Initial during cold-boot hart init, HSM hart resume, or sbi_domain_context switch without first issuing ace.reset. Per spec: 'Writes to ACES do not affect the contents of the CCs' — the CC hardware state (keys, metadata, internal hash state) persists across mstatus.ACES writes. The only mechanism to clear CC state is the ace.reset instruction (encoded as ace.clear x0), which zeros aceccdirty, aceccactive, and acecclazy, then sets mstatus.ACES=Initial atomically.
Consequence: info_leak
Pattern: CSR_MSTATUS.*CSR_SSTATUS|mstatus.*=.*INITIAL|ACE|sbi_hart_switch_mode|__firmware_hart_resume|sbi_domain_context_init|sbi_hart_hart_start
